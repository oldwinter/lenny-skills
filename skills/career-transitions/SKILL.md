# Career Transitions

使用来自 76 位成功转换过角色、行业和职业阶段的产品领导者的框架，帮助用户应对职业变化。

## 如何提供帮助

当用户请求 career transition 相关帮助时：

1. **识别 move 类型** - 判断他们是在寻找新 role、新 function、新 stage，还是完全新的 path
2. **理解 motivations** - 挖掘推动变化的 "pushes"（不满）和 "pulls"（吸引）
3. **评估 readiness signals** - 帮助他们判断现在是否是合适时机，还是需要更多准备
4. **设计 experiments** - 建议低风险方式，在完全投入前测试假设

## 核心原则

### Progress 比 pay 更重要
Bob Moesta: "Over 50% of the people who got new jobs didn't get more money. It's a lie. It's about progress. It's about what do they want to learn? What skills do they want to get?" 找到你的 "metric of progress"，明确成长对你具体意味着什么。

### 使用 Genie Framework
Graham Weaver: "What if you had one wish... whatever you throw yourself into, it's going to turn out great." 把失败恐惧从方程里拿掉，识别你真正想要什么。再从 10 年后的成功结果倒推。

### 通过关系建立 serendipity
Gokul Rajaram: "Great careers are built by knowing a lot of people doing great work so they know and want you on their teams, and just waiting for serendipity and then seizing it." 与聪明且做优秀工作的人建立关系，比线性晋升路径更重要。

### 跟随 intuition，而不是 spreadsheet
Ami Vora: "The thing that has consistently served me is to do the thing that feels right, go to the place that feels like home, work with the people who feel like my friends." 选择那些让你觉得自己很 "lucky" 能身在其中的角色。

### 三个月 sabbatical 是可实现的
Paul Millerd: "A three month sabbatical is much more attainable than people think... if you're assuming you're going to work continuously in adulthood, that's about 500 months." 把 sabbatical 看成整个职业生涯中的很小一部分，会让它更可行。

### 像做产品一样经营 career
Gibson Biddle: "It's just a lot like building a product. You have theories and hypotheses, you find ways to experiment with them." 在做出重大承诺前，用小 experiments 测试 career hypotheses。

### Internal moves 更容易
Anneka Gupta: "Doing it within the same company is a lot easier than trying to switch companies and switch jobs at the same time because you've already built credibility." 做 role transition 时，利用已有关系和 domain knowledge。

### 平衡 learning 和 impact
Deb Liu: "You can have the most impact in the job you know the best, but then you stop learning... How do you keep going back and forth so that you're not going straight up?" 在 mastery roles 和 "newbie" roles 之间切换，避免停滞。

### 加入 winning teams
Matt MacInnis: "As an early career product manager... you should join a winning team. I want to hear what they learned from being part of a winning team." 高增长公司通常比挣扎中的公司提供更多学习机会。

## 帮助用户的问题

- "如果成功被保证，你会追求什么？"
- "你是在逃离什么，还是奔向什么？"
- "你上一次真正被工作点燃是什么时候？"
- "如果不需要赚钱，你会做什么？"
- "你最欣赏哪三个人的 career？"
- "你能运行的最小 experiment 是什么，用来测试这条 path？"

## 需要提醒的常见错误

- **早期过度优化 compensation** - 与后期 executive compensation 相比，早期收入影响很小
- **因为 inertia 留在原地** - 观察你的环境是否仍提供 learning，还是你已经成了 "boiling frog"
- **追逐 title** - 在输家公司拿高级 title，常常不如在市场领导者那里做稍低级别角色
- **跳过 sabbatical buffer** - 在高强度角色之间直接切换，会把旧文化包袱带到新环境
- **忽视 "habitat" fit** - 在一个环境失败，不代表你缺技能；也可能只是环境不合适
