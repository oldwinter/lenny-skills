# Working Backwards

使用来自 12 位产品领导者的框架和洞察，帮助用户应用 working backwards methodology。

## 如何提供帮助

当用户请求 working backwards 相关帮助时：

1. **从 customer 开始** - 在讨论 solutions 前，询问 customer 是谁、有什么 problem
2. **写 press release** - 指导他们起草描述 finished product 的 mock press release
3. **开发 FAQ** - 帮助他们预判并回答关于 feasibility 和 strategy fit 的 internal questions
4. **测试 three criteria** - 评估 idea 是否足够大、strategically appropriate，并且有 viable plan

## 核心原则

### 从 customer problem 开始
Ian McAllister: "Working backwards is all about the problem and starting there. Teams that do it wrong don't work backwards - they have something they want to build." 避免给 pre-existing solution 硬套 problem。在定义 solution 前，先写 problem paragraph。

### 相信 results 会跟随 value
Bill Carr: "Jeff would say, we took it as an article of faith. If we served customers well, things like revenue and share price would follow." 从 ideal customer solution 往回推，并相信 business metrics 会随之而来。

### 使用 backcasting 摆脱 present
Christopher Lochhead: "Standing in that future, five years out, looking back to the present, what did we do to make this different future happen? That's how you unshackle yourself from the past." 想象 radical future，然后在不受 current limitations 束缚的情况下 work backward。

### PR/FAQ forces clarity
Bill Carr: "We're going to start by writing a press release describing the feature in a way that speaks to the customer. The idea better jump off the page as something a customer will really need." 在 build 任何东西前，先写 problem paragraph、solution paragraph、customer quote 和 leadership quote。

### 评估三件事
Ian McAllister: "One, is it a big idea? Two, is it something we should be doing? Three, is there a legitimate plan to succeed?" 成功的 product proposal 必须通过这三个 tests。FAQ 证明是否存在 legitimate plan。

### 先定义 ideal state
Melanie Perkins: "Column B is work from this dream reality and work backwards from how to achieve that." 先想象 'castle on the hill'，也就是 mythical perfect experience，再从今天到这个 vision 建立 ladder rungs。

### Align the entire machinery
Anuj Rathi: "You're working backwards from an entire machinery at a particular day that is working. What will be the machinery we would have created so that this product is successful?" Working backwards 包含 GTM strategy、organizational alignment 和 stakeholder buy-in，而不只是 product 本身。

### 定义 theoretical ideal
Nilan Peiris: "What is the theoretical minimum cost for moving money? What is the theoretical maximum speed? Not just make it instant, make it cheap, but what actually is the lowest it could possibly be?" 定义 best possible outcome，并从那里 work backward，而不是从 incremental improvements 出发。

## 帮助用户的问题

- "Customer 是谁？用他们自己的话说，他们的 problem 是什么？"
- "如果你今天为这个 product 写 press release，headline 会是什么？"
- "Customer quote 会如何描述这件事如何改变了他们的生活？"
- "这个 idea 是否大到值得做？你的 company 是否适合 build it？"
- "能 address the elephant in the room 的 FAQ 是什么？"
- "Theoretical ideal state 是什么样，而不只是 improvement？"

## 需要提醒的常见错误

- **Starting with the solution** - 最常见 failure 是先有 solution，再给它 retrofitting problem
- **Vague customer benefit** - Press release 必须带着清晰 customer value 跳出页面
- **Skipping the FAQ** - 关于 feasibility 和 strategy 的 internal questions 对 honest evaluation 很关键
- **Incremental thinking** - 从 ideal work backward，而不是从 status quo 的 small improvements 出发
- **Only product focus** - Working backwards 应该包含 GTM、organizational alignment 和 stakeholder management
