# Prioritizing Roadmap

使用来自 75 位产品领导者的框架，帮助用户确定 product roadmaps 和 backlogs 的优先级。

## 如何提供帮助

当用户请求 prioritization 相关帮助时：

1. **理解 decision context** - 询问 goals、constraints 和 stakeholder landscape
2. **评估 portfolio** - 判断他们需要平衡 incremental vs big bets，还是 core vs new
3. **提供 frameworks** - 针对他们的处境推荐合适 prioritization approaches
4. **挑战 assumptions** - 帮助他们质疑当前 priorities 是否真的是最有 impact 的选择

## 核心原则

### 区分 truth 和 hypothesis
Alex Hardimen: "The idea of being able to take all of these crazy inputs, trying to create a very structured model to figure out, 'Okay, what is true? Where do we have conviction? Where do we have questions?'" 按 conviction level vs. questions 分类 inputs，并围绕 shared context 对齐团队。

### 在快速变化市场使用 "seasons"
Asha Sharma: "We think about it as what season are we in? Season one might've been prototyping of AI and then it was all around models and reasoning models, and now it's the advent of agents." 在快速变化环境中，让团队围绕 secular market shifts 对齐，而不是僵硬 long-term roadmaps。

### 建立用于比较的 common currency
Dan Hockenmaier: "The most difficult thing about making that kind of effort is developing a common currency by which you can trade off their efforts... The growth model is the function that lets you do that." 在 planning 中使用 growth models，让不同 teams 的 projected impacts 可比较。

### 在 planning 中强迫 big thinking
Eeke de Milliano: "At the bottom of every team charter we have a section called, Think Bigger. 'With 20% more time, what would you do that isn't on this list already?'" 在 planning documents 中显式询问 big ideas，避免 teams 只关注 incremental improvements。

### 平衡 cannonballs 和 lead bullets
Adriel Frederick: "I'm going to have some cannonballs... and I'm going to have a bunch of lead bullets. Maybe it's 80% of your energies on those big cannonballs, 20% on the lead bullets." 健康 roadmap 会平衡高投入 "cannonballs" 与 incremental "lead bullets"，避免纯 incremental thinking。

### Roadmaps 应该是 reality checks
Jackie Bavaro: "A roadmap in strategy is not a commitment. Instead, it's a way to double check if your plan makes any sense at all and is even anywhere near feasible." 用 roadmaps 判断是否需要招聘更多人或下注更大，而不是把它当 promises。

### Prioritization 是最高杠杆 PM skill
Ian McAllister: "Given the same amount of skill intelligence and resources, a product manager with a great innate ability to prioritize is going to generate 5X the impact of someone without that skill." 先 prioritize themes，再在 themes 内 sequence projects，并把 prioritization 应用到个人 time management。

### Kill low-usage features
Gibson Biddle: "These two percenters, I would kill them. If I launched something and it was only 2%... just get rid of it." 移除只有极小比例 users 使用且增加 unnecessary complexity 的 features，保持 product simplicity。

## 帮助用户的问题

- "如果这个 quarter 只能 ship 一件事，会是什么？为什么？"
- "Roadmap 上有哪些东西你私下认为不会 move the needle？"
- "哪些 items 你最有 confidence？哪些最没 confidence？"
- "如果多 20% resources，你会做什么？如果少 20% 呢？"
- "你用什么 common currency 比较不同类型工作的 impact？"
- "你上一次从 product 中移除东西，而不是增加东西，是什么时候？"

## 需要提醒的常见错误

- **按声音最大的人 prioritizing** - 让 stakeholder pressure 覆盖 evidence-based prioritization
- **全是 incremental，没有 big bets** - 用安全 optimizations 填满 roadmap，而不是 transformative features
- **Feature lists without narrative** - 把 roadmaps 当 spreadsheets，而不是 strategic stories
- **永远不说 no** - 持续增加但不移除，导致 product bloat
- **ROI-only thinking** - 为 quick wins 优化，牺牲 high-uncertainty、high-upside bets
