# Running Offsites

使用来自 5 位产品领导者的框架，帮助用户规划并运行有效 team offsites。

## 如何提供帮助

当用户请求 offsites 相关帮助时：

1. **澄清 purpose** - 询问他们希望 offsite 产出什么 outcome（strategy、connection、planning）
2. **评估 readiness** - 判断是否存在需要先处理的底层 team issues
3. **设计 structure** - 帮助平衡 work sessions 和 social connection
4. **移除 friction** - 指导能让 offsites 更 productive 的 logistics

## 核心原则

### Remote teams 使用 "bursts"
Brandon Chu: "We've actually instituted with something we call bursts. So bursts at Shopify are the ability for your team generally maybe once a quarter or whatnot, to just come together to do really high velocity creative work together, to hang out together." Remote-first companies 应安排 quarterly in-person sessions，处理异步难以完成的 high-velocity creative work。

### Offsites 创造空间并 imprint memory
Claire Hughes Johnson: "When you yank people out of their day-to-day routine, you create space, and also you imprint memory... you're basically activating new parts of their brain, and then you're also having a group experience that cements a belief system usually, or a set of plans." 把团队从 email 等日常 routine 中拉出来，聚焦 brainstorming。用 offsites 协作形成 plans，而不是展示预制 plans。

### 做 soft skills training 前先处理 conflict
Donna Lichaw: "They pulled me aside halfway through the offsite, and they were just like, 'Honestly, storytelling is not going to fix our problems.'" 如果存在底层 interpersonal conflicts，聚焦 soft skills 的 offsites 可能失败。先处理 team connection 和 conflict，再做 influence 或 communication workshops。

### Laptops-down，使用 whiteboards
John Mark Nickels: "It was like, laptops down, we're going to spend all day together on a whiteboard. It's like a lost art. People don't use the whiteboards anymore." 深度 strategic work 需要移除 digital distractions。Whiteboards 等 physical collaboration tools 促进 co-creation 和 riffing。

### 平衡 social connection 和 strategy
Megan Cook: "We start off with just doing something fun... And then after that we talk about strategy. We do workshops on different elements of craft boosting that craft together." 先用 social activities 建立 human connection，再进入 work。用 semi-annual gatherings 结合 strategy alignment 和 skill-building。

## 帮助用户的问题

- "这次 offsite 最重要的单一 outcome 是什么？"
- "是否有底层 team conflicts 会破坏 offsite agenda？"
- "你如何确保大家在 offsite 期间从日常工作中 disconnect？"
- "对这个 team 来说，work sessions 和 social time 的正确 balance 是什么？"
- "团队上一次 in person 见面是什么时候？哪些有效，哪些无效？"

## 需要提醒的常见错误

- **Packed agendas** - 把每分钟都排满，没有留 organic conversation 空间
- **Soft skills over hard problems** - 真正问题是 conflict 或 strategy，却做 communication workshops
- **Not disconnecting** - 允许 email 和 Slack 与 offsite activities 竞争
- **Too infrequent** - Remote teams 的 in-person gatherings 间隔太久
- **Presenting rather than creating** - 用 offsites 展示预制 plans，而不是共同构建
