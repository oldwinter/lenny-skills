# Positioning & Messaging

使用来自 58 位产品领导者和 marketers 的框架，帮助用户打造有说服力的 product positioning 和 messaging。

## 如何提供帮助

当用户请求 positioning 和 messaging 相关帮助时：

1. **理解 target audience** - 询问他们具体想触达谁，以及这些人关心什么
2. **识别 competitive frame** - 判断 customers 正在拿哪些 alternatives 与他们比较
3. **找到 differentiated value** - 帮助他们阐明 offering 中独特真实且有价值的东西
4. **测试 message resonance** - 引导他们使用反映 customers 如何描述自身问题的语言

## 核心原则

### Positioning 决定一切
Arielle Jackson: "I really believe that positioning dictates so much of your marketing and should always be the first thing you do. I had a student in my last class... He goes, I'll never write a line of code without doing positioning first." Positioning 是 marketing 的 foundational element，应该先于 product development 和所有其他 marketing activities。

### 弱 positioning 会制造 pipeline friction
April Dunford: "Weak positioning hurts you in the early stages of pipeline in that people don't really get what you are, so they're not responding to your marketing the way they should. And you'll get this sluggishness in the middle of your pipeline." 如果 customers 在 pitch 中要求 sales reps "back up and start over"，这是 positioning problem 的信号。

### Communication 由 receiver 定义
Gina Gotthilf: "Communication isn't about being able to convey a message, it's about being able to convey a message in a way that the listener receives it, and understands it, and remembers it." 有效 communication 由 listener 是否理解和记住 message 来定义，而不只是你是否发送了 message。

### 对 press 使用 pattern matching
Emilie Gerber: "You can get so in the weeds with your own messaging that you want to set up this massive problem statement... but if you're very straightforward and you're pattern matching, it's generally actually going to work." 对 press 来说，直接的 pattern matching 比复杂 problem statements 或 trend stories 更有效。

### Brand 设置 expectations
Jessica Hische: "The cover of the book should tell people what to expect from the thing that they're about to engage with." Brand 的 visual identity 应该像共生预告，设置正确 expectations 和 product experience tone。

### 从 struggling moment 开始
Bob Moesta: "A struggling moment causes demand. And you start to realize that in some cases that struggling moment exists and can exist for a long time and nobody solved it." 好 positioning 从理解 users 感到 stuck 的具体 context 开始，而不是从 product features 开始。

### Differentiation 需要 sacrifice
April Dunford: "You can't be everything to everyone." 真正 positioning 需要明确决定你不适合谁，以及你不提供什么。

## 帮助用户的问题

- "当 customer 切换到你的 product，他们是从什么切换过来的？之前在做什么？"
- "如果你的 product 明天消失，你最好的 customers 最想念什么？"
- "最开心的 customers 向朋友推荐你时会怎么说？"
- "谁明确不是你的 product 的好 fit，为什么？"
- "你明确在 against 哪个 competitive alternative 做 positioning？"
- "公司里每个人都能一致描述你们做什么吗？"

## 需要提醒的常见错误

- **Feature-first messaging** - 以 product 做什么开头，而不是 customers 能达成什么
- **Positioning by adjectives** - 使用 "powerful" 或 "easy-to-use" 这类每个 competitor 都会声称的词
- **试图 appeal to everyone** - 因害怕错过机会而拒绝缩小 target audience
- **Complex problem statements** - 过度解释 problem，而不是使用直接 pattern matching
- **Company-centric language** - 使用 prospects 完全不理解的内部 jargon 或 product names
