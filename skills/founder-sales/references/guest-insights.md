# Founder Sales - 所有嘉宾洞察

*16 位嘉宾，28 次提及*

## April Dunford
*April Dunford 2.0*

> "40 to 60% of B2B purchase processes end in no decision. If you scratch on that data, the majority of those aren't saying, 'Well, I'm not making a decision to buy something new because the old thing we were doing is better.'... the majority of those is they couldn't figure out how to make a choice confidently."

**洞察：** B2B sales 中的 primary competitor 往往不是其他 software vendors，而是 customer indecision，以及对 high-stakes mistake 的恐惧。

**战术建议：**
- 聚焦帮助 buyer confidently make a choice，而不只是 pitching features。
- 扮演 guide，帮助 buyer navigate the market 并理解 options。
- 认识到对 overwhelmed buyer 来说，'no decision' 是最安全、risk-free 的 path。

*时间戳：00:00:00*

---

> "If a customer is indecisive, throwing FOMO into the mix makes it worse. So they're less likely to close the deal if you throw that in. And the idea is that they're already stressed out and you're just putting more stress on them by pressuring them."

**洞察：** 对 indecisive buyers 使用 fear of missing out（FOMO）可能适得其反，加重 paralysis；应改为聚焦 de-risking the decision。

**战术建议：**
- 对 paralyzed 或 indecisive prospects 避免 high-pressure FOMO tactics。
- 提供帮助 customer buy 的 tools，例如 money-back guarantees 或 implementation support。
- 将 large deals 拆成 smaller、less intimidating pieces，降低 perceived risk。

*时间戳：00:26:49*


## Bob Moesta
*Bob Moesta*

> "Instead of trying to base the sales process on how we want to sell, we need to actually design the sales process on how they want to buy. And it seems like it's the same thing, but they're actually really, really different things."

**洞察：** Sales processes 应按 buyer 自然的 decision-making timeline 设计，而不是按 company internal sales targets 设计。

**战术建议：**
- 将 buyer timeline map 为六个 phases：first thought、passive looking、active looking、deciding、first use 和 ongoing use。
- 根据 buyer 所处 specific phase 调整 demos（例如 passive looking 阶段用 educational stories，deciding 阶段用 trade-off choices）。

*时间戳：00:14:24*


## Bret Taylor

**洞察：** Founding 本质上是 sales role；你必须向 investors、talent 和 customers sell the vision。

**战术建议：**
- 采用 flexible identity，把 sales 视为 building 的 core responsibility

*时间戳：00:14:17*


## Dalton Caldwell
*Dalton Caldwell*

> "The Collison Install is what often happens with customers, is that they say, 'Yes, I want you buy your product.' And then they do not implement it, they just go quiet. There's no implementation and this is very bad if you're selling software to someone... they would just install Stripe into the customer's website... they basically would not go away until you finish the implementation of Stripe."

**洞察：** Early sales success 需要 'white glove' service，确保 customer 在初始 'yes' 后真的 implements and uses the product。

**战术建议：**
- 在 product fully implemented 前，不要认为 sale 已结束
- 主动为 customer manually install 或 set up software，确保 activation
- 保持 persistent 和 available，帮助 customers 走完 implementation 的 'last mile'

*时间戳：00:45:53*

---

> "I think if you find people that are really excited and you do line up customers, that is a great green light that it is time to do a startup, right? That can get you down the path... Build it once you have some conviction and then you're like, 'Oh, I think I would have a customer. I think that at least one person would use this thing I want to build.'"

**洞察：** Founders 在 commit to building full product 前，应尝试通过至少一个 customer pre-sell 或 validate demand。

**战术建议：**
- 在写大量 code 前，尝试 pre-sell solution
- 寻找 potential customers 给出的 'green lights'，建立 conviction

*时间戳：01:11:20*


## Geoffrey Moore
*Geoffrey Moore*

> "Shut the God damn laptop. Just don't open it and start with... 'We're here because we've been working with some people in your industry and we understand there's this really serious problem... and we believe that your company might have it, is that true?'"

**洞察：** 面向 pragmatists 的 early sales 应是 diagnostic 的，并完全聚焦 customer pain，而不是 product demos。

**战术建议：**
- 在 initial diagnostic meeting 中避免打开 laptop 或做 demo。
- 提出 probing questions，让 customer 'self-diagnose' their problem。

*时间戳：00:42:35*

---

> "Your goal in that call is to get them to talk as much as possible and for you to take notes. And by the way, this is a place where you probably still want to remember to use a pen because you actually want them to see you writing down their words, because that means, 'Okay, he's listening.'"

**洞察：** Active listening 和 physical note-taking 能与认为自己 problems unique 的 pragmatic buyers 建立 trust。

**战术建议：**
- 使用 physical pen and paper，让 customer 看到你在记录他们的 specific words。
- 在 discovery phase 最大化 customer 的 'talk time'。

*时间戳：00:48:00*


## Gustaf Alstromer
*Gustaf Alstromer*

> "The problem is, which anyone who hasn't done sales before that joined YC, they realize this, is that if you take the average customer group in the world, 90% are not early adopters... Those 10 percents are the early adopters. They're The ones that you actually want to reach. But that means you have to reach 10 to find one."

**洞察：** Founder-led sales 是一个 numbers game：筛出愿意押注 new product 的 early adopters。

**战术建议：**
- 做 high-volume outreach，找到少数具有 high problem intensity 的 users

*时间戳：00:24:25*


## Jen Abel
*Jen Abel*

> "Founder led sales is really that first milestone that a startup goes through on the commercial side, which is, how do I go out and get my first few customers? ... the founder is the product."

**洞察：** 在 early stages，在 brand 或 product fully established 前，founder 的 novel insight 和 subject matter expertise 是 interest 的 primary drivers。

**战术建议：**
- 利用 founder 在 hierarchy 中最高阶的 status 获得 market access。
- 利用 founder 在 conversations 中发现 'budding moments' 的能力，refine product vision。

*时间戳：00:03:29*

---

> "If you can focus the messaging in a way that speaks to something that has a bit of shock value, or is counterintuitive, you'll get them to continue reading. ... relevancy. I think that matters even more so than personalization right now."

**洞察：** Effective cold outreach 依赖 immediate relevancy 和 counterintuitive hook，而不是 generic personalization。

**战术建议：**
- 将 outreach emails 控制在最多 3-4 句，确保 mobile 上无需 scrolling。
- 让 message 聚焦正在解决的 problem，而不是 solution。
- 使用带有 'shock value' 或 counterintuitive 的 line，让 recipient 暂停下来。

*时间戳：00:13:20*

---

> "Before you buy any tool, don't even think about tools. ... Can you manually find 30 people that you want to spend 15 to 20 minutes writing a rock solid note to?"

**洞察：** 在尝试用 automation tools scale 前，manual lead generation 和 outreach 是识别 correct parameters 与 messaging 的必要步骤。

**战术建议：**
- 手动 research 30 个 prospects，识别 team size、industry 或 role duration 等 commonalities。
- 手动 test messaging，观察哪些 roles 和 problems 带来 highest response rates。

*时间戳：00:23:28*

---

> "Founder led sales is not about revenue on day one. It is about learning as fast as humanly possible to get to that pulse, so that you can earn the right to sell."

**洞察：** Early founder-led sales 的 primary goal 是 market research 和 learning，而不是 immediate revenue generation。

**战术建议：**
- 将 early sales calls 当作 research sessions，收集 market 如何 perceive the problem 的 intel。
- 对 early-stage startup 的身份保持 vulnerable，换取 prospects 更 honest 的 feedback。

*时间戳：00:35:47*

---

> "Get the second call booked on the first call. Pull up calendars. Look at calendars. Who else should be invited?"

**洞察：** Maintaining momentum 需要在 current call 仍进行时就 secure next meeting。

**战术建议：**
- 在 first call 结束前，请大家 pull up calendars。
- 识别 next session 中应包含的 other stakeholders。

*时间戳：00:42:25*

---

> "It's qualification. Qualification because if you spend your time on the wrong leads, that equates to a zero. ... it's never a bottom of funnel problem. It's always qualification."

**洞察：** 大多数 sales failures 实际上是 funnel 顶部 qualification 的 failures，而不是底部 closing issues。

**战术建议：**
- 继续前，验证 prospect 今天是否在 measuring 或 managing the problem。
- 停止追逐那些 'just being nice' 但没有 intent to buy 的 leads。

*时间戳：01:10:25*

---

> "I don't use a tool. The thing about AI tools is they're all pulling from the same databases. I want to email someone not in the database that's getting hit by a million folks. I want to take a back door in, not the front door where everyone else is trick or treating."

**洞察：** 对 high-value enterprise deals，manual、highly personalized outreach 比使用同一批 saturated databases 的 automated AI-driven tools 更有效。

**战术建议：**
- 使用 LinkedIn 上的 visual cues（例如在某个 role 中待了多久）customize notes。
- 发送 manual、one-sentence emails，让它感觉 human，并区别于 automated spam。

*时间戳：01:01:18*


## Jonathan Lowenhar
*Jonathan Lowenhar*

> "Sales. And this is the codification of a playbook. How are we having the conversation? How are we doing discovery? How are we handling objections? How are we doing demo? How are we moving to close?"

**洞察：** Early sales 的目标，是将 founder 的 'magic' codify 成 others 可 follow 的 repeatable playbook。

**战术建议：**
- Document 能带来 successful qualifications 的 specific discovery questions。
- 创建处理 common objections 和 running demos 的 standard process。
- 确保 playbook 足够 structured，让 'non-founder' account executives 能 execute。

*时间戳：01:03:37*


## Marty Cagan
*Marty Cagan 2.0*

> "If they're a real product manager and they're worried about value and viability, that is the founder's job. So the founder should be doing that and needs to be doing that, and it usually causes conflict if they bring in a real product manager too soon."

**洞察：** 在 early stages，founder 必须 own 'value and viability' functions，以避免 organizational conflict。

**战术建议：**
- 在 team 达到大约 20-25 engineers 前，founders 应担任 primary product person。
- 如果 founder 仍是 product value 和 business viability 的 primary driver，避免过早 hiring PM。

*时间戳：01:15:53*


## Pete Kazanjy
*Pete Kazanjy*

> "The point is is that you can't outsource that behavior, the founder's got to do that stuff. A lot of people ask me that question, "Well, I suck at sales," or, "I'm afraid to talk to people," or, "Interfacing with non-friendly parties makes me uncomfortable." The way to think about it is... it's going to be way easier for you to get good, or minimally viable good, at selling by having interactions with non-friendly parties and having commercial conversations and asking for money in exchange for the value delivery."

**洞察：** Founders 不能 outsource early sales，因为他们需要 direct feedback loop 来 validate product 和 message。

**战术建议：**
- 在亲自 closed first couple dozen customers 前，不要 hire VP of Sales
- 将 early sales calls 用作 customer development，refine product-market fit
- 聚焦成为 selling 上 'minimally viable' 的人，而不是 master

*时间戳：00:11:11*

---

> "One, it's going to help you with your product development because you're not going to have that abstracted, for sure, two, you're going to be the person who's going to figure out how to talk about it in an effective way, and then three, it's going to make it such that you can package that up such that other human... Because that's the way that B2B startups scale... by adding more salespeople who then have customer-facing meetings with prospects."

**洞察：** Founder-led sales 有三个 purposes：product refinement、discovering the pitch，以及为 future hires 创建 'source code'。

**战术建议：**
- 在 selling 过程中 document discovery questions、slides 和 scripts
- 在 hiring others 前，识别 sales process 中 repeatable 的 'while loop'

*时间戳：00:13:55*


## Raaz Herzberg
*Raaz Herzberg*

> "We ended up selling before we had a seller's team. We ended up, almost in some ways, always being behind, right? Okay, I'm closing contracts with people, I haven't hired my first salespersons... if you can't do it one time end to end, and you're the core, core group, the chances of just bringing somebody from the outside to solve that problem, it's wishful."

**洞察：** Founders 必须亲自 close 前几百万 ARR，确保 sales motion repeatable 后再 hiring sales team。

**战术建议：**
- 不要 hire salesperson 来修复一个 isn't landing 的 message；core team 必须先证明它 works。
- 在 building formal sales org 前，目标是通过 founder-led sales 达到 couple million ARR。

*时间戳：00:21:23*


## Robby Stein
*Robby Stein*

> "My co-founder and I just basically got the contact of Scooter Braun... we literally went immediately to the airport. I just remember just basically going straight to the airport, flying to LA meeting with him... Intense urgency usually wins over thinking about it for a long time."

**洞察：** Early-stage success 往往需要 extreme scrappiness 和 immediate action 来 secure high-leverage partnerships。

**战术建议：**
- 用 'intense urgency' close deals。如果明天有 meeting，立刻上飞机。
- 早期 target 'tastemakers' 或 influential users，解决 cold start problem。

## Sahil Mansuri
*Sahil Mansuri*

> "Stop using email. Email is where deals go to die. Text message is where deals get done... I don't take the introer off the thread... It holds the person's feet to the fire to actually show up for the meeting."

**洞察：** High-stakes sales 需要 text messaging 这类 high-touch communication，并利用 mutual connections 带来的 social pressure。

**战术建议：**
- 通过 text message 而不是 email 请求 introductions
- 让 introducer 留在 text thread 上，确保 meeting accountability
- 如果 warm lead 变安静，持续 follow up（最多 9 周）

*时间戳：00:56:03*

---

> "I personally work with Microsoft. So I have a little bit of insight in this. Here's what your employees think about you. Here's what Mark's approval rating looks like versus others, et cetera, et cetera.' This whole research report, I kind of broke out some highlights, a couple screenshots, attached the report and said, 'Hey, I'd love to discuss this with you sometime.'"

**洞察：** 通过提供 deep、customized research，改变 prospect 对自身 business 的 perspective，从而 close major accounts。

**战术建议：**
- 对 prospect company 做 deep sentiment analysis 或 data research
- 使用 high-leverage subject lines（例如 'Mark's approval rating'）
- 用他们从别处拿不到的 insights target highest-level executive

*时间戳：01:03:18*

**洞察：** B2B growth 应始于 founders 和 early team 亲自 handling sales，在 scaling 前理解 customer。

**战术建议：**
- 在 hiring dedicated sales staff 前，确保 early team 知道 how to sell
- 将 founder-led sales 作为理解 customer needs 的 foundation

## Todd Jackson
*Todd Jackson*

> "I tend to gravitate towards the, 'I want to sell it before I build it,' because I really want the signal from customers and I want that to be the guide and the oxygen that drives what I'm building."

**洞察：** 在 building full product 前 sell the vision 或 mock-ups，能提供必要 signal，确保 product 满足另外三个 Ps（Persona、Problem、Promise）。

**战术建议：**
- 在写 extensive code 前，用 Figma mock-ups secure first 5-10 customers。
- 根据 product type 判断 demo 所需的 'fidelity'（例如 workflow tools 用 mock-ups，analytics tools 用 real data）。

*时间戳：00:34:25*


## Jeanne Grosser
*Jeanne Grosser*

> "Wait until you're around a million in ARR. When you have a repeatable process... as a founder you want to stay deeply connected to customers and get it to a scale and get it to a point where you use the word, there's some repeatability there."

**洞察：** Founders 应 lead sales，直到达到 roughly $1M ARR，并拥有 documented、repeatable ICP。

**战术建议：**
- 在能 define ICP（例如 'startups with <100 employees building SaaS'）前，不要 hand over sales。

*时间戳：00:29:05*

---

> "Excellent salespeople typically will talk well under half the time in a conversation because they're out asking questions, probing often helping a customer arrive at conclusions on their own. And so learning how to do five why's, go deep rather than immediately going into problem solving mode."

**洞察：** Effective sales discovery 关乎 deep listening 和 probing，而不是 immediate problem-solving。

**战术建议：**
- 使用 'five whys' technique，并围绕 customer 的 questions 继续提问，以 uncover deeper pain。

*时间戳：00:54:39*
