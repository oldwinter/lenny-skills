# Writing Job Descriptions

使用来自 6 位产品领导者的框架和洞察，帮助用户写出有效 job descriptions。

## 如何提供帮助

当用户请求 job descriptions 相关帮助时：

1. **先定义 success** - 询问 hire 后 12 个月 success 是什么样，而不是他们会做哪些 tasks
2. **识别 spike** - 判断这个人需要 excel 的一两个 areas（不是 everything）
3. **围绕 progress 来写** - 围绕要创造的 progress frame role，而不是列 arbitrary tasks
4. **考虑 signal** - 讨论文案是否会吸引或排除 right candidates

## 核心原则

### 从 12-month success 开始
Jonathan Lowenhar: "Start with, it's 12 months later, you hired the person, 12 months have gone by, you're clinking champagne because of how great it's been. What's changed about the business?" 用一年后的 business impact 定义 success，而不是 responsibilities list。

### Job descriptions are made up
Bob Moesta: "Job descriptions are made up. They're literally just made up - a list of stuff the manager wants plus stuff they don't want to do." 关注这个 role 能带来的 'progress'，而不是 arbitrary feature requirements。

### 识别他们应该在哪里 spike
Lauren Ipsen: "Trying to determine where this person should major and minor, where they should spike. Is this someone that's going to lean into design efforts or operate like a very senior PM?" 定义 required specific competencies，而不是寻找什么都能做的 generalist。

### 把 requirements framed as outcomes
Bob Moesta: "Don't tell me I need Excel, PowerPoint, and Word skills. Tell me what I'm going to do with those. Tell me you're going to need to build PowerPoints for executive alignment." 用时间本应代表的 specific capabilities 替代 'X years of experience'。

### 基于 candidates iterate
Jason Shah: "Taking a product mindset where I meet people and don't know exactly what role they're going to fill. A product mindset on hiring and iterating on it based on the candidates you're meeting." 把 job descriptions 当作会随 market reality 演化的 iterative documents。

### 有意使用 polarizing language
Anton Osika: "Long hours, high pace, candidates must thrive under high urgency. Those seeking comfortable work need not apply." High-signal、polarizing language 能 filter 出适合 specific environments 的 candidates。

### Codify emerging roles
Peter Deng: "I asked her to write up a job description of what this thing is. There's something magical here. The role was model designer." 请 high-performing individuals 写自己的 ideal job description，用来识别 new functional needs。

## 帮助用户的问题

- "如果这次 hire 极其成功，12 个月后 business 会有什么不同？"
- "这个人绝对必须 great at 的一件事是什么？"
- "你描述为 requirements 的内容中，哪些其实只是其他东西的 proxies？"
- "你想用这套 language 吸引谁？又想 filter out 谁？"
- "这个 role 是在解决 specific problem，还是没人想做的 tasks collection？"
- "这个人在前 6 个月的 'making progress' 会是什么样？"

## 需要提醒的常见错误

- **Lists of tasks instead of outcomes** - 关注这次 hire 会带来什么不同，而不是他们每天做什么
- **Requiring generalists** - 识别 specific spikes；想找什么都擅长的人，通常谁也找不到
- **'Years of experience' as a proxy** - 这无法告诉 candidates 他们实际需要做什么
- **Static descriptions** - 见过 candidates、理解 market 后，iterate role definition
- **Bland language** - Generic job postings 会吸引 generic candidates；要 specific，甚至 polarizing
