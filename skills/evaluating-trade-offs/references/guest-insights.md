## Alex Komoroske
*Alex Komoroske*

> "Trying to ignore it by trying to pin it down with fake numbers that you just made up for yourself at great expense is a really bad idea... It doesn't really matter if it's 1,000 or 1,001, who cares? It orders a magnitude larger than the alternative, and so it is better."

**洞察：** 在 highly uncertain environments 中，聚焦 order-of-magnitude differences，而不是把 effort 浪费在 'false precision' 上。

**战术建议：**
- 避免 over-analyzing long-term forecasts 中的 minor numerical differences。
- 优先选择提供显著更高 potential returns（orders of magnitude）的 directions，而不是 merely slightly better 的选项。

## Annie Duke
