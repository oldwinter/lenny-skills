# Evaluating Candidates

使用来自 94 位产品领导者的实战框架，帮助用户做出更强的 hiring decisions。

## 如何提供帮助

当用户请求 evaluating candidates 相关帮助时：

1. **理解上下文** - 询问他们处于哪个阶段，例如 screening resumes、reviewing work samples、conducting references、making final decisions，以及正在招聘什么 role
2. **应用相关原则** - 使用下面的框架帮助他们思考 decision
3. **挑战 assumptions** - 对 pedigree bias 或只凭 gut feel 等 shortcuts 提出挑战
4. **帮助结构化流程** - 基于原则建议 interview questions、reference check approaches 或 evaluation rubrics

## 核心原则

### Reference checks 胜过 interviews
Shishir Mehrotra: "I generally value the reference check over interview signals. Those people worked with this person for years—what you get from 30 minutes of interviewing doesn't compare." 在最终决策中优先参考 references，并深入追问与 candidate 密切共事过的人。

### 为 team balance 招聘，而不是找 unicorns
Adam Fishman: "The goal is not to find someone who's 11/10 on everything—that person doesn't exist. Create a well-rounded team by hiring to fill gaps in your portfolio." 开启 role 前，先 map 团队当前 strengths 和 weaknesses。

### 使用 paid work trials
Elena Verna: "We do 2-3 day paid work trials to see candidates in action—how they handle chaos and lack of clarity. This company is not for everybody." Work trials 能揭示 interviews 无法揭示的东西：一个人真实如何运转。

### Agency 优先于 experience
Albert Cheng: "High agency and 'clock speed' are better predictors than deep experience. Sometimes experience is a crutch, especially when the ground is shifting fast." 寻找在正式渠道之外主动采取行动的 candidates。

### 先 structure，再 intuition
Annie Duke: "If you use intuition after a structured evaluation—not before—you drastically improve your hit rate." 在 gut feel 影响决定前，先完成 rubric。

### 寻找 world-class strengths，而不是没有 weaknesses
Ben Horowitz: "We're investing in strength, not lack of weakness. Does this person have a world-class strength that can beat anybody? Surround them with people who cover their gaps."

### 测试对 chaos 的舒适度
Adam Grenier: "Comfort with chaos and willingness to do things they haven't done in 15 years are huge signals. People from traditional environments often can't handle startup unpredictability."

### 识别 T-shape
Adam Grenier: "Every senior hire has a T-shaped career. Find their deep expertise (the vertical), then ask how they'll cover areas where they're weaker."

### 观察 interview 之外的行为
Albert Cheng: "High agency shows up outside formal interviews—the questions they ask, whether they've actually tried your product, the energy in their scheduling emails."

### 避免 pedigree shortcuts
Austin Hay: "School prestige or resume gaps are bad shortcuts. Investigate gaps—they may represent intense self-directed learning."

### 使用 Bar Raiser model
Bill Carr (Amazon): "Have someone outside the hiring manager's chain run the debrief meeting with veto power. This counteracts urgency bias and keeps the bar high."

### 为 person-product fit 招聘
Brian Tolkin: "Match the candidate's specific background to the product's needs. A technical product needs a technical PM. Avoid hiring generalists for roles where specific context drives success."

### 强制 stack-ranking
Bangaly Kaba: "Ask candidates to stack-rank five skills from strongest to weakest. It reveals self-awareness and opens deeper conversation about their actual capabilities."

### Mission alignment 留住 talent
Benjamin Mann (Anthropic): "People stay when they're mission-oriented. They get offers from Meta but don't leave because their best case here is affecting humanity's future."

## 帮助用户的问题

用这些问题诊断他们需要哪类帮助：

- "你现在处于流程的哪个阶段？"
- "你当前团队缺少什么，而这个人需要补上什么？"
- "你做 reference checks 了吗？学到了什么？"
- "你用了 structured rubric，还是主要凭 gut feel？"
- "你见过他们实际工作吗，还是只 interview 过？"
- "他们的 world-class strength 是什么？"
