# Content Marketing

使用来自 23 位产品领导者的框架，帮助用户构建有效的 content marketing；这些领导者曾在 Notion、First Round、The Pragmatic Engineer 等公司搭建内容引擎。

## 如何提供帮助

当用户请求 content marketing 相关帮助时：

1. **识别目标** - 判断 content 是为了 SEO、brand building、lead generation，还是 thought leadership
2. **寻找 content-market fit** - 帮助他们识别内容要解决的具体 anxieties 或 needs
3. **选择合适格式** - 让 content format 匹配 creator strengths 和 audience preferences
4. **为 consistency 设计** - 在追求 virality 前，先建立可持续 publishing cadence

## 核心原则

### 先验证 search demand
Meltem Kuran: "If people aren't asking this question to Google, you can write all the content you want, it doesn't matter, nobody's going to find it." 在投入创作前，确认人们真的在搜索这个 topic。

### 创造 content-market fit
Camille Ricketts: "The way that you think about product market fit, you have to think about content market fit... What is it that they need to get promoted? What is it that they need to avoid failure?" 把 content 当作解决具体职业焦虑的 product。

### Content 必须是 painkiller
Camille Ricketts: "Painkillers always win. Can your content be a painkiller? Can it help people out of situations that are causing them a lot of pain?" 聚焦解决具体 pain points，而不是提供泛泛的 "vitamins"。

### 用 human voice 直接发声
Lulu Cheng Meservey: "Going direct means that the founder or executive has to be speaking from themselves. First person, speaking in a human voice authentically. You see them make mistakes, you see them be vulnerable." Corporate accounts 容易失败，personality 更容易胜出。

### 终结 Google search
Meltem Kuran: "The main thing to think about is, is the Google search over? If someone reads your content, are they going back to Google to continue reading more or is the Google search over?" 写出能完整满足 user intent 的内容。

### 投资 evergreen assets
Julian Shapiro: "I'm spending as many hours going back and rewriting old blog posts as I am writing new ones. I consider everything I write to be evergreen." 定期更新旧内容，而不是只追逐 news cycles。

### Consistency 胜过 production value
Sriram and Aarthi: "The most important thing is just get started and do something every single day... it builds muscle, it gets you familiar with the medium." 刚开始时，daily reps 比 polish 更重要。

### AI-assisted，而不是 AI-generated
Ethan Smith: "The future of content is clearly AI-assisted. Clearly, you and I will be using AI to help us write, so it's not no AI at all, but it's not 100% generated with AI." 人类 expertise 提供 search engines 奖励的 "information gain"。

## 帮助用户的问题

- "人们真的在 Google 上搜索这个 topic 吗？"
- "这篇 content 解决了哪种具体职业焦虑？"
- "这是 painkiller 还是 vitamin？"
- "这个 content 背后的 human face/voice 是谁？"
- "如果它不能带来 traffic，你仍然享受创作它吗？"
- "你能承诺至少一年都坚持这个 format 吗？"

## 需要提醒的常见错误

- **为没有 demand 的主题写作** - 创作没有人搜索的内容
- **Keyword stuffing over intent** - 优化关键词，却没有真正回答问题
- **Corporate voice** - 用没有面孔的 brand accounts 发布，而不是用真实姓名的人发声
- **发布不稳定** - 追逐 virality，而不是建立 sustainable cadence
- **100% AI generation** - 缺少需要人类 expertise 才能提供的 "information gain"
