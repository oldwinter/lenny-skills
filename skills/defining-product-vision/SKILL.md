# Defining Product Vision

使用来自 101 位产品领导者的框架，帮助用户创建有感染力的 product visions；这些领导者曾在 Notion、Airbnb、the New York Times 等公司定义愿景。

## 如何提供帮助

当用户请求 product vision 相关帮助时：

1. **澄清 scope** - 判断他们需要 vision（长期 aspiration）、strategy（如何取胜），还是 roadmap（构建什么）
2. **聚焦用户的未来** - 帮助描述 5-10 年后的世界，而不是产品 features
3. **测试 specificity** - 对不会改变行为的模糊 taglines 提出挑战
4. **让它可视化** - 鼓励使用 prototypes 和具体 artifacts，而不是抽象 documents

## 核心原则

### Vision 不是 tagline
Melissa Perri: "I once asked all the executive team at a healthcare company, what's the vision for this company? And they said, to be the backbone of healthcare. And I said, what does that mean? And they couldn't elaborate." Vision 必须具体描述公司会在 5-10 年里 manifest 出什么。

### 强 vision 的四个标准
Ebi Atawodi: "It has to be lofty, it has to be realistic, it has to be devoid of any tech or limitations of today, and it has to be grounded in a very clear and potent problem." 在忽略当前技术限制的同时，平衡 aspiration 和 attainability。

### 把 radical vision 藏进 familiar utility
Ivan Zhao (Notion): "Our realization is actually let's hide our vision, which is everybody can create their software, in the form factor that people do care. So what kind of tool do people use every day? Productivity software." 用熟悉、高实用性的 form factor 包装激进的长期 vision。

### Vision 描述用户世界，不描述你的产品
Ben Williams: "The vision is the nirvana state that you aim to enable for your users and customers in five to 10 years... It should not mention your company, your product, or anything solution related at all." 用 'In the future...' 开头，保持长期视角。

### 从 'what' 走向 'why'
Chip Conley: "Ultimately, we came up with the idea that we were in the belong anywhere business. Airbnb was not in home sharing, we were in belonging anywhere." 有力量的 vision 会超越功能性的 'what'，进入情绪性的 'why'。

### 使用 visual artifacts
Cam Adams (Canva): "We need mock-ups. We need prototypes. You need to get that idea out of your head and present it in a visual form that helps you talk about and communicate about it." 用有形 prototypes 传达 vision，而不只是抽象文档。

### Vision 指导 decentralized decisions
Ami Vora: "If we all agree that the feeling of something should be, I'm sitting in Dolores Park with my friends on a sunny Saturday, then people will just naturally build something that feels more consistent." 使用 emotional metaphors 建立共同理解，让团队不需要 micromanagement 也能做一致决策。

### Strategy 强迫选择
Chandra Janakiraman: "Product strategy sits between the mission and vision and the plan... It forces choice to deploy scarce resources to generate maximum impact." Strategy 是 aspirational vision 与 tactical roadmap 之间的连接组织。

## 帮助用户的问题

- "如果你无法具体阐述 vision，它到底是 vision，还是只是 tagline？"
- "如果你们成功了，5-10 年后的世界是什么样？"
- "哪个用户问题强烈到让你觉得它仍然存在很不可接受？"
- "团队成员能只凭你的 vision 做出好决策吗？"
- "如果没有今天的技术限制，你的产品会是什么样？"
- "你希望用户感受到什么 emotional state？"

## 需要提醒的常见错误

- **模糊 taglines** - 例如 "Be the backbone of healthcare"，但没有具体阐述
- **把 feature lists 当 vision** - 描述你会构建什么，而不是用户生活会变成什么样
- **提到 product 本身** - Vision 应描述用户世界，而不是 solution
- **受当前 constraints 限制** - 好 vision 会超越今天的技术限制
- **没有 behavioral change** - 如果 vision 不改变团队优先级，它就没用
