# Stakeholder Alignment

使用来自 88 位产品领导者的框架和洞察，帮助用户实现 stakeholder alignment。

## 如何提供帮助

当用户请求 stakeholder alignment 相关帮助时：

1. **理解 situation** - 询问 stakeholders 是谁、需要做什么 decision，以及 resistance 来自哪里
2. **识别 motivations** - 帮助他们理解每个 stakeholder 关心什么，以及各自对 success 的定义
3. **结构化 approach** - 指导 pre-alignment、communication sequence，以及如何 present options
4. **准备 objections** - 帮助预判 Most Obvious Objections (MOOs) 并准备 responses

## 核心原则

### 理解他们如何思考，而不只是他们想什么
Hilary Gridley: "What's more helpful than understanding what your CEO thinks is understanding how your CEO thinks." 分析 leader feedback 中反复出现的 themes，把它转化为 actionable principles，而不是追逐具体 opinions。

### Present options with a recommendation
Matt LeMay: "Options and a recommendation is the magic formula. When you present a single option, people just start poking holes." 总是提出至少三个 options，带上清晰 trade-offs 和一个 specific recommendation，以促进 constructive discussion。

### 先在 strategy 上获得 pre-alignment
Shreyas Doshi: "If you have a real product strategy that everybody is aligned with, a lot of the nonsense we do with annual planning goes away." 在 formal planning seasons 前先取得 strategy alignment，避免 endless friction。

### 用 curiosity 处理 disagreements
Ami Vora: "She would respond, 'Fascinating, you have to tell me more why you think that.'" 当你听到强烈 disagree 的 idea，先假设对方掌握了你还没有的信息。

### 区分 disagreement 和 misunderstanding
Tomer Cohen: "Are you disagreeing or misunderstanding? If misunderstanding, let's spend the time. If disagreeing, let's stop wasting time arguing." 请对方用自己的话复述你的 point of view，以验证 understanding。

### Trust 通过 consistency 建立
Ian McAllister: "Trust is the currency of a product leader. Trust is built by repeatedly setting and meeting expectations." 说真话，按承诺的内容和时间 launch，并立即 own mistakes。

### 用 concentric circles 做 communication
Lulu Cheng Meservey: "Go out in concentric circles. Each circle assumes the inner circle knows better." 在 communication sequence 中不要跳过任何一圈（Team -> Stakeholders -> Leadership -> External）。

### Relentlessly repeat messages
Zoelle Egner: "I repeated the same three talking points 5,000 times. You just have to get used to saying the same stuff." 在多种 formats 中扮演 'repeater-in-chief'，因为 people 需要多次接触才会内化 message。

### 学会 business constraints
Marty Cagan: "You have to know how it's marketed, how it's sold, how it monetizes. You have to convince stakeholders you understand what the issues are." 通过展示对 legal、finance、sales 和 compliance constraints 的深刻理解来 build trust。

### 把 requests framed as trade-offs
Wes Kao: "I can design this PDF for you. That means the page redesign will have to wait until later this week." 当被要求做 new tasks 时，明确说明哪些 current work 会被 deprioritized，而不是简单说 yes 或 no。

## 帮助用户的问题

- "对这个 stakeholder 来说，success 具体是什么样？他们最关心什么？"
- "你是否识别了 broader meeting 前需要 1:1 alignment 的 'gatekeepers'？"
- "你预期的 Most Obvious Objection 是什么？你会如何 address？"
- "是否有 repeated disagreements 暗示这是 strategy 未对齐，而不是 tactical issue？"
- "你会如何跨不同 modalities（written、verbal、visual）communicate？"
- "你希望他们接受哪些 trade-offs？你是否已经明确说出来？"

## 需要提醒的常见错误

- **Presenting a single option** - 这会招致 criticism；总是 present multiple options with trade-offs
- **Skipping pre-alignment** - 把 buy-in 当成单次 high-stakes meeting，而不是 collaborative journey
- **Framing as pushback** - 把 disagreement 看成 conflict，而不是围绕 business success 对齐的 constructive effort
- **Assuming one communication is enough** - Messages 需要跨 multiple channels and formats 重复
- **Not understanding motivations** - 在不知道对方个人关心和害怕什么的情况下尝试 align
