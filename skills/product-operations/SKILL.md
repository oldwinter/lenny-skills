# Product Operations

使用来自 5 位产品领导者的框架，帮助用户搭建并扩展 product operations functions。

## 如何提供帮助

当用户请求 product operations 相关帮助时：

1. **理解 pain points** - 询问当前 product processes 中什么正在出问题
2. **评估 organizational scale** - 判断他们是否到了需要 dedicated product ops 的阶段
3. **定义 scope** - 帮助澄清 product ops 应该 own 什么，以及 PMs 应保留什么
4. **设计 systems** - 创建能 enable product teams、但不制造 bureaucracy 的 processes

## 核心原则

### Product ops 连接 product 和 operations
Brian Tolkin: "One solution to that problem, our solution at the time was to start up a new function called product operations who had accountability and reported into operations but physically sat with and operated much like a member of the product team." Product ops 起源于 centralized product teams 和 distributed operations 之间的 bridge，确保 product decisions 考虑 operational reality。

### Product ops 创建让 product teams thriving 的 systems
Christine Itwaru: "Product operations for a VP or a head of product or a product manager is the creation of some system that allows you to thrive or allows your team to thrive in product management." 这个 function 的目标是构建 enable product management 的 systems，而不是自己做 product management。

### Product ops 提升 scaling velocity
Geoff Charles: "We invested early on in product operations... they basically are tasked with a lot of the work that needs to get done to continue shipping products and scaling product development." Product ops 处理 release management、enablement 和 operational tasks，避免 PMs 被这些工作从 core work 中拉走。

### Product ops informs, not decides
Melissa Perri + Denise Tilles: "Product operations does not take away decision making rights from the product manager. It's there to inform them." Product ops 提供 insights 和 infrastructure，但不做 product decisions；decision 仍属于 PMs。

### Product ops 帮助 scale 中的 standardization 和 insights
Melissa Perri: "Product management at scale is really hard, and that's where product operations comes in. So what it does is it helps you get the right insights to the team, and then help standardize those outputs and those check-ins." 这个 function 聚焦 standardizing roadmaps、scaling user research，以及在组织内浮现 data insights。

## 帮助用户的问题

- "哪些 operational tasks 正在把 PMs 从 product work 中拉走？"
- "Sales、support 和 operations 的 insights 今天如何进入 product teams？"
- "如果每个 PM 都有标准化 processes 和 tools，会有什么不同？"
- "Product 和其他 functions 之间最大的 coordination gaps 在哪里？"
- "你的 product org 从什么时候开始遇到 scale 问题？"

## 需要提醒的常见错误

- **Product ops as PM work** - 要求 product ops 做 product decisions，而不是 enable PMs
- **太早投资** - Product org 还没大到需要 product ops，就开始搭建这个 function
- **Process for process's sake** - 创建会拖慢团队而不是 enable 团队的 standardization
- **Siloed from product** - Product ops 汇报给 operations，却与 product teams 缺少紧密连接
- **Ownership 不清晰** - Product ops owns 什么、PMs owns 什么模糊不清
