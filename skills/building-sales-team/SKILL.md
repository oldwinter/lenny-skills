# Building Sales Team

使用来自 14 位从零到规模化搭建销售团队的产品领导者的框架，帮助用户构建并扩展高绩效销售组织。

## 如何提供帮助

当用户请求搭建 sales team 相关帮助时：

1. **理解所处阶段** - 询问当前 revenue、deal size、sales motion（inbound vs outbound），以及 founders 是否仍在亲自 close deals
2. **识别触发条件** - 在建议招聘前，判断他们是否已经有可重复的 sales motion 和可衡量的 win rate
3. **让 profile 匹配 motion** - 帮助他们为具体 buyer 和 channel 找到合适的销售 archetype
4. **围绕 validation 设计结构** - 指导他们如何成对招聘、设计 compensation structures，以及何时增加 management

## 核心原则

### 先证明 repeatability，再招聘
Pete Kazanjy: "You can reliably, at a pretty okay win rate, so maybe 15% or 20% or 25%, turn first meetings into eventual customers." 等 founders 达到统计上有意义的 win rate（50-100 次 at-bats）后，再招聘 salespeople。

### 永远成对招聘
Jason Lemkin: "You need to hire one rep and you've got to hire two because otherwise, there's no A-B test. You have to A-B test humans." 同时招聘两个 reps 可以建立 performance baseline，并验证流程是否不依赖单个人才。

### 把 sales hires 推迟到 Series A
Jen Abel: "Seed is all about experimentation and proving out that experiment, and then obviously series A is about exploiting that learning." Founder 必须亲自 close deals 来学习市场，然后再委托出去。

### 让 profiles 匹配你的 motion
Annie Pearl: "In those early days, it's more inbound in nature, and so the type of sales reps you might need are not necessarily going to be outbound, heavy hunting sales reps." PLG/inbound 需要 "grower" profiles，outbound 需要 "hunter" profiles。

### 寻找 founder-like sellers
Jen Abel: "I always say you need people that can cosplay a founder, which is selling the vision, getting through walls to get the deal done." 早期 sales hires 应该是 product-obsessed 的 "pirates and romantics"，能适应不同 deal structures。

### Sales hires 必须通过 PM test
Jeanne Grosser: "If you are an account executive in my org and I put you in front of 10 engineers at our company, it should take them 10 minutes to figure out you aren't a product manager." 在技术公司，sales 必须有深厚产品知识。

### 不要太早招聘 VP
Jason Lemkin: "You need two sales reps hitting quota closing deals before you're ready to hire a manager for them. Almost all VPs of sales, their job is to take you from rep three to 300." 在两个 sales reps 稳定达成 quota 前，不要先招 manager。

### 把 PLS pilot 和 quota pressure 分开
Elena Verna: "Attach to yourself a pilot AE, so account executive, or an SDR and see how that works... not under pressure of top-down quota relief." 在传统 sales engine 之外试验 product-led sales，不要一开始就压上 top-down quota relief。

## 帮助用户的问题

- "你当前从 first meeting 到 closed deal 的 win rate 是多少？"
- "你是在卖给 arms-length prospects，还是主要靠 warm intros？"
- "你的 average deal size 和 sales cycle length 是多少？"
- "你的 motion 主要是 inbound 还是 outbound？"
- "你是否已经有两个 reps 能持续达成 quota？"
- "你的 target buyer persona 是 department head 还是 C-level？"

## 需要提醒的常见错误

- **在 founder-led repeatability 前招聘销售** - 如果 founder 不能稳定 close deals，salespeople 也做不到
- **第一个就招 VP of Sales** - 从两个 pioneer AEs 开始，而不是先招多年没亲自卖过的 manager
- **sales profile 和 motion 不匹配** - 如果你卖给 RevOps managers，找擅长卖给 CIOs 的 Oracle reps 可能行不通
- **单点失败式招聘** - 一个 rep 成功可能只是运气；两个 reps 才能验证流程
- **忽视 50% failure rate** - 一半早期 sales hires 都不会成功，要提前按这个概率规划
