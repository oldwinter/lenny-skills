# Enterprise Sales

使用来自 9 位产品领导者的框架，帮助用户推进 enterprise sales；这些领导者曾在 Stripe、Superhuman 等公司 close large enterprise deals。

## 如何提供帮助

当用户请求 enterprise sales 相关帮助时：

1. **识别阶段** - 判断他们处于 prospecting、discovery、procurement，还是 closing
2. **Map buying committee** - 帮助识别 champion、economic buyer 和其他 stakeholders
3. **处理 indecision** - 聚焦克服客户对搞砸的恐惧，而不只是制造 FOMO
4. **推进 procurement** - 指导他们处理 administrative 和 compliance requirements

## 核心原则

### 40-60% deals 死于 indecision
Matt Dixon: "Our analysis showed that anywhere between 40 and 60% of the average salesperson's qualified pipelines will be ultimately marked as closed loss, no decision." 大多数 deals 不是输给 competitors，而是输给 customer inertia 和 fear。

### 识别并武装 champion
April Dunford: "Typically we have between five and seven people involved in what we call making the decision. By far the most important persona is what we call the champion - this person's job is to get consensus and champion the deal across everybody." 给 champion 准备面向 IT、Legal 和 Economic Buyer 的材料。

### FOMU 胜过 FOMO
Matt Dixon: "The omission bias is the fact that people don't want to be blamed for making decisions that lead to a loss. People are okay with missing out. They are not okay with messing up and being blamed." 处理 Fear of Messing Up，而不只是 Fear of Missing Out。

### 让 procurement 的工作变简单
Jen Abel: "When you get to procurement, you're going to have to do all the work. Make their job easy. Give me the forms that you need to fill out. I'll fill them out for you." 主动承担 administrative burden，避免被边缘化。

### POCs 是为了 business cases，不是 demos
Madhavan Ramanujam: "The POC should be framed as the entire goal of the POC is to create a business case, period, full stop. It is not to demonstrate product functionality." 把 POCs 当作共同建立 ROI models 的协作练习。

### PLG 到 enterprise 是 escalator
Elena Verna: "Product-led sales converts the usage that you've generated via self-serve into a sales opportunity and it attaches a salesperson to close a much larger contract." 识别从 individual use case 到 enterprise-level solution 的 escalator。

### Under-promise, over-deliver
Matt Dixon: "What great salespeople do is they know that while they'll stand by those claims, they try to under-promise and over-deliver. Build your business case around a 5X improvement, then let's set up to over-deliver against that." 用保守、100% 可实现的数字构建 business case。

### 使用 JOLT method
Matt Dixon 提出四步克服 indecision：Judge the level of indecision、Offer a firm recommendation、Limit exploration by building trust、Take risk off the table by de-risking the deal。

## 帮助用户的问题

- "account 里的 champion 是谁？他们需要什么才能成功？"
- "什么会让这个 deal 死于 'no decision'？"
- "buyer 对搞砸这件事最大的恐惧是什么？"
- "你是否识别了 final signatory，以及他们关心什么？"
- "你是在 collaborative 地构建 business case，还是只是在 presentation？"
- "你能做什么来 de-risk buyer 的这个 decision？"

## 需要提醒的常见错误

- **只依赖 FOMO** - 增强 urgency，却没有处理对失败背锅的恐惧
- **忽略 champion** - 直接卖给 economic buyer，而不是武装内部 advocate
- **被动 procurement** - 等 buyer 自己推进内部流程，而不是替他们做
- **Demo-focused POCs** - 把 proof of concepts 当技术测试，而不是 business case exercises
- **Best-case projections** - 用乐观数字构建 business case，最终只会制造失望
