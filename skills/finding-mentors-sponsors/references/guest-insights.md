## Ada Chen Rekhi
*Ada Chen Rekhi*

> "If you're thinking about getting a coach... talk to two or three different coaches. And really get a sense of their vibe and how well you get to know them... half of people that founder their coach literally went with the first coach that they talked to."

**洞察：** Coaching 最有效的前提是 strong personal connection 和 'vibe'，而不只是 credentials 匹配。

**战术建议：**
- 在确定某位 coach 前，先 interview 多位 coaches。
- 在 hyper-growth phases、sensitive interpersonal conflicts 或 specific skill acceleration 中使用 coaches。
- 对 fundraising pitches 或 writing 等 short-term goals，考虑 'niche' coaches。

## Bangaly Kaba

**洞察：** 建立由 multiple mentors 组成的 'stable'，即使某个人 unavailable，也能确保 consistent support 和 diverse perspectives。

## Bret Taylor
*Bret Taylor*

## Jules Walter
*Jules Walter*

> "make the smallest ask possible, which is the opposite of what 95% of people do. 95% of people is like, 'Hey, I've never met you... Can we set up a call?' That's like a big ask. What I do... reached out in the evening and I was like, 'Hey, thanks so much for speaking today... Is there an example of product that you think was created with this approach?' Something he could answer in literally two minutes via email?"

**洞察：** 通过 tiny、specific、几乎不费力回答的 email requests 开始，再通过 follow-ups 构建 relationship，从而获得 high-level mentors。

**战术建议：**
- 从一个能在两分钟内通过 email 回答的 specific question 开始。
- 一定要 circle back 给 mentor，说明你如何应用了他们的 advice，以及结果是什么。
- 寻找不仅是 experts、还具备清晰解释 process 能力的 mentors。
- 通过询问 mentors 当前面对什么 problems，并主动提供 help 来 reciprocate。
