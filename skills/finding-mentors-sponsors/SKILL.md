# Finding Mentors & Sponsors

使用来自 19 位产品领导者的方法，帮助用户建立有意义的 mentor 和 sponsor relationships。

## 如何提供帮助

当用户请求寻找 mentors 或 sponsors 相关帮助时：

1. **澄清目标** - 询问他们需要 advice（mentor）、advocacy and opportunities（sponsor），还是 accountability（coach）。这些是不同关系，需要不同做法
2. **识别潜在人选** - 帮助他们思考谁在理想路径上领先 2-3 年、谁解决过他们的具体问题、谁拥有 organizational influence
3. **设计 approach** - 引导他们提出小而具体的 asks，而不是正式提出 "will you be my mentor"
4. **建立关系** - 辅导他们如何带着结果 follow up、提供 reciprocal value，并长期维护连接

## 核心原则

### Sponsors 对职业加速比 mentors 更重要
Christopher Miller: "Mentors are great... but I would actually describe those folks as being sponsors and advocates, people who were willing to put up capital, whether that's professional, social capital to bet on you." 区分 advice-givers（mentors）和 opportunity-creators（sponsors）。通过 coachable 和替他们交付结果，与潜在 sponsors 建立 trust。

### 永远不要正式问别人能否做你的 mentor
Gibson Biddle: "Don't ask a person to be your mentor. That's really awkward. First, identify them... then find ways to be helpful. Everybody needs help." 通过先提供 value 来有机建立 mentorship，而不是提出正式请求。

### 从最小 ask 开始
Jules Walter: "Make the smallest ask possible... 'Is there an example of product that you think was created with this approach?' Something he could answer in literally two minutes via email." 从很小、很具体、几乎不费力的问题开始，再通过展示你应用了建议来发展关系。

### 承认你不知道什么
Chip Conley: "Brian would go to experts and say, 'I don't know what the hell I'm doing.'... I appreciated that a guy who had a lot of hubris could also have the humility to say, 'I want to learn more about this.'" 无论 seniority 如何，诚实承认 knowledge gaps 是向 mentors 学习的有效方式。

### 建立一组 mentors，而不是只有一个
Bangaly Kaba: "It's actually better to have a stable of mentors. You want to have three or four. And ideally, what you do is you meet with each one of them once a month on a different Friday." 与不同 mentors 在不同周固定会面。提出 mentor 请求时，用具体 challenge 描述，而不是请求泛泛 mentorship。

### 问 "why" 提取 frameworks，而不只是 answers
Bret Taylor: "When you ask for advice, don't just ask what to do but why. Be an obnoxious two-year-old kid, why? Why? Why?" 把建议拆解成底层 frameworks，避免把对方具体 anecdotes 误用到你的不同处境。

### 你的问题并不独特
Elena Verna: "Don't think that you have unique problems. You don't... Your problem has been solved by somebody." 通过 LinkedIn 或 X 联系其他公司的 peers，询问他们如何解决具体 growth challenges。也可以聘请 advisors 为新 initiative 提供 structural frameworks。

### Mentorship 可以是一组 collection，而不是单一关系
Ami Vora: "I had everything I needed, people were so kind and generous, but I didn't recognize it that way because we talk about it differently." 在脑中建立不同 leaders 的 "emulators"，调用他们的具体 skillsets。不要被寻找一个完美 mentor 的压力困住。

### 用 peer coaching circles 扩展支持
Deb Liu: "I'm in a lean-in group and we support each other... coaching circles give you an opportunity to learn from each other and to get peer coaching." 加入或组建同职业阶段 peers 的 coaching circle，共享挑战并判断问题是个人的还是情境的。

### 研究 "dead or distant mentors"
Phyl Terry: "Warren Buffett is my mentor, he just doesn't know it... if you really study that moment and study what Jobs did, it can inform your decisions." 选择一位你尊敬的 leader，研究他们在关键职业时刻的具体行动。优先读 primary sources，而不只是 biographies。

### 提供书面问题作为低 friction 选项
Paul Millerd: "If we can't jump on a call, can I send you a list of questions?" Cold outreach 时，提供发送 written questions 的选择，而不是直接请求 live call，可以提高回复率。

## 帮助用户的问题

- "你是在寻找某个问题的 advice，还是需要有人 advocate for you 并创造 opportunities？"
- "在你想走的 path 上，谁领先你 2-3 年？"
- "你想在哪个具体问题或 decision 上获得帮助？"
- "你的组织里谁有 influence，可以为你 'go to bat'？"
- "你能为潜在 mentors 提供什么回报价值？"
- "有没有人会督促你对自己的 goals 负责？"

## 需要提醒的常见错误

- **问 "Will you be my mentor?"** - 这种正式请求很尴尬，也给双方压力。通过具体 asks 自然建立关系
- **寻找一个完美 mentor** - 没有人会完全匹配你的 path。建立一组有不同 strengths 的 mentors
- **只要 advice，从不展示结果** - 一定要回头展示你如何应用了对方 guidance。这会建立 trust 并维护关系
- **混淆 mentorship 和 sponsorship** - Mentors 给 advice；sponsors 用自己的 professional capital 押注你。知道自己需要哪一种
- **太早提出 big asks** - 先从两分钟 email 能回答的问题开始，再请求 calls 或 ongoing relationships
