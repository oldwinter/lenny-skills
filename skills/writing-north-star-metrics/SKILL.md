# Writing North Star Metrics

使用来自 27 位产品领导者的框架和洞察，帮助用户定义 North Star metric。

## 如何提供帮助

当用户请求 North Star metrics 相关帮助时：

1. **理解 value** - 询问 product 向 users 交付的 specific value 是什么（不是 revenue 或 internal activity）
2. **测试 simplicity** - 确保 company 中任何人都能理解并讨论这个 metric
3. **检查 actionability** - 确认 teams 可以通过自己的工作实际影响这个 metric
4. **添加 guardrails** - 帮助他们识别能防止 gaming 的 countervailing metrics

## 核心原则

### 衡量 delivered value，而不是 captured value
Itamar Gilad: "The North Star metric measures how much value we create for the market. WhatsApp measured messages sent because every message is incremental value. Airbnb used nights booked." 选择一个 tracking 提供给 customer 的 core utility 的 metric，而不是 business extraction。

### 避免 lagging indicators
Jess Lachs: "Retention is a terrible thing to goal on. It's almost impossible to drive in a meaningful way in a short term. Find a short-term metric you can measure that drives a long-term output." 识别对 experimentation 敏感、且与 long-term goals 相关的 proxy metrics。

### Simple beats sophisticated
Jess Lachs: "If people understand it, if they have an intuition around it, if it's something people can talk about across the company, it's going to be a much better metric than your made up composite score that nobody understands." 避免带 complex coefficients 的 composite metrics。

### 从 customer perspective 衡量
Jeff Weinstein: "What was the value we're trying to produce for the customer, and can we measure it from their perspective? We suggested 'companies with zero support tickets.'" 基于 friction 的缺失或 success moments 的出现来定义 metrics。

### High-level mission 简化 decisions
Hari Srinivasan: "Everything at LinkedIn is a very connected ecosystem, but decisions aren't difficult because we're all here to help people connect to economic opportunity." 清晰的 company-wide mission 是 product decisions 的 ultimate tie-breaker。

### 给 metrics 取 evocative names
Jeff Weinstein: "Picking metric titles that make you feel something. 'Companies with zero support' - the brevity and customer mindset built into the chart name can become currency inside the company." 使用 simple、evocative names，而不是 technical database field names。

### 精确定义并 codify
Manik Gupta: "Everyone will talk about the same metric but have different nuances. What is a daily active user? Pick a definition, instrument it, codify it. No confusion." Metrics 只有在 precise definitions 和 accurate instrumentation 支撑下，才能驱动 alignment。

### 选择 hard activation metric
Lauryn Isford: "An activation rate that falls in a lower percentage range, maybe 5-15%, is better than a high percentage because it means there's likely much higher correlation with long-term retention." 只有少数 users 达到的 high-bar activation metrics 往往更有价值。

### Periodically revisit
Lauryn Isford: "A North Star metric should be a measure of what you plan to do. Revisit North Star metrics every 6-12 months to ensure they still align with business goals." 如果 strategy 需要，要愿意 shift metrics。

### 避免把 revenue 当作 North Star
Sean Ellis: "I think monthly purchases is great because it maps to value people are getting. Units of value from the customer perspective is more important than overall revenue." Revenue 应该是把事情做对后的产物，而不是 day-to-day guiding metric。

## 帮助用户的问题

- "哪个 specific moment 代表 user 从你的 product 获得 value？"
- "你公司里的 non-technical person 能理解并讨论这个 metric 吗？"
- "Teams 能否在一个 quarter 内通过自己的工作实际影响这个 metric？"
- "如果你 game 这个 metric，会发生什么？什么会坏掉？"
- "这个 metric 衡量的是 delivered to users 的 value，还是 extracted from users 的 value？"
- "这是 leading indicator，还是很难推动的 lagging one？"

## 需要提醒的常见错误

- **Revenue as North Star** - Revenue 是 outcome；关注驱动它的 customer value
- **Complex composite metrics** - 如果你无法简单解释，teams 就无法 rally around it
- **Lagging indicators like retention** - 找到能预测 retention 的 leading metrics
- **Gaming vulnerability** - 添加 countervailing metrics，防止伤害 users 的 optimization
- **Undefined terms** - 在精确定义 counts 之前，'Active user' 没有意义
