# Platform Strategy

使用来自 24 位构建并扩展过 platforms 的产品领导者的框架，帮助用户设计并执行 platform business strategies。

## 如何提供帮助

当用户请求 platform strategy 相关帮助时：

1. **理解 platform type** - 澄清他们是在构建 marketplace、API platform、ecosystem，还是 developer platform
2. **识别 network effects** - 帮助理解 platform 的哪几侧会相互创造 value
3. **评估 lifecycle stage** - 判断他们处于 moat-building、opening，还是 closing phase
4. **为 trust 和 governance 设计** - 帮助思考管理 platform participants 的 rules

## 核心原则

### 把 internal platforms 当作 products
Camille Fournier: "Platform engineering is not just maintaining cloud infrastructure... platforms are products, ultimately. You should be thinking about how do I create coherent offerings that make this company more productive?" Internal platforms 需要 dedicated product management，并聚焦 user（developer）productivity，而不只是 technical elegance。

### 理解四阶段 platform lifecycle
Brian Balfour: "The four steps are essentially, one is I call a Step Zero. It's the conditions of the market have been met. Step One is about a moat, Step Two is about a platform opening, and Step Three is about the platform closing for control and monetization." Platforms 会经历从 market consensus 到 closing for monetization 的可预测 lifecycle。

### 通过清晰 interfaces 降低 cognitive load
Jeremy Henrickson: "The more you can bake into a clear platform, it reduces the decision-making complexity for everyone who's working on the domain part of the problem." 定义良好、interfaces 清晰的 platform 会降低 individual teams 的 cognitive load，从而简化 product development。

### 寻找 compounding dynamics
Alex Komoroske: "Anything that is shaped like an ecosystem that has some kind of network effect... you have to know what you're looking for and find the dynamics of a thing that if it worked would work at an accelerating rate." Platform success 来自识别 "gardening" opportunities，也就是带有内在 compounding loops、能自我增长的 projects。

### 构建 systems，而不是 features
Aparna Chennapragada: "The way I think about how we are positioned and what we do with GitHub is... So it's a system, not just a product or a set of features." 长期 platform defensibility 来自构建 comprehensive system 和 context repository，而不是单个 feature 或 tool。

### 基于 signals 逐步投资
Alex Komoroske: "Invest incrementally in ecosystem projects only as you receive signals of utility and adoption." 在获得 demand 和 usage patterns 证据前，不要对 platform initiatives 下大赌注。

## 帮助用户的问题

- "你的 platform 哪一侧更难 acquire？那可能就是你应该先聚焦的地方。"
- "当没有其他 participants 时，你的 platform 能为单个 user 提供什么 value？"
- "你处于 platform lifecycle 哪个 stage：building the moat、opening，还是 closing？"
- "你如何防止 supply side 被 commoditized 或绕过你？"
- "你的 platform 中有哪些会随着增长而加速的 compounding dynamics？"
- "你会执行哪些 governance rules？如何处理 disputes？"

## 需要提醒的常见错误

- **在 vacuum 中构建 platforms** - 没有基于真实 product 和 developer needs 迭代
- **把所有 participants 同等对待** - 没识别 power users 和 high-quality suppliers 应得到不同对待
- **跳过 moat phase** - 在建立 defensibility 前就 opening platform
- **Feature thinking over systems thinking** - 构建 point solutions，而不是带 context 的 comprehensive systems
- **signals 前过度投资** - 没有 utility 和 adoption 证据，就对 platform initiatives 下大赌注
