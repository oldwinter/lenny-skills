# Conducting User Interviews

使用来自 43 位产品领导者的技巧，帮助用户开展更好的 discovery conversations，并提取真实洞察。

## 如何提供帮助

当用户请求 user interviews 相关帮助时：

1. **理解目标** - 询问他们想学到什么，例如 validating a problem、testing a solution、understanding behavior、pricing research
2. **帮助准备** - 建议问题、提醒常见错误，并帮助他们找到合适 participants
3. **辅导技巧** - 分享如何获得诚实、有用回答，而不是礼貌性 validation
4. **帮助分析发现** - 协助把学到的内容综合成可执行洞察

## 核心原则

### 收集 stories，而不是 opinions
Teresa Torres: "Interviewing is a grossly underestimated skill. If you're not collecting rich stories, you won't identify opportunities." 不要问 "What do you like?"，而要问 "Tell me about the last time you..."

### 只访谈已经采取行动的人
Bob Moesta: "I only talk to people who've already tried to make progress. What made them try? Ignore 'bitching' (complaining)—look for 'switching' (actual behavior change)." 忽略抱怨，寻找真实行为变化。

### 观察，而不只是询问
Gustaf Alstromer: "The best way to understand problem intensity isn't asking—it's watching. Have them screen share and walk through their daily workflow. Look for pain they've normalized." 让对方 screen share 并走一遍日常 workflow，寻找他们已经习以为常的痛点。

### 避免 pitching
Jeff Weinstein: "Don't start with 'Hi, I'm the CEO of X, we do Y, let me show you a demo.' What a wasted opportunity. Listen first. Use silence to let them open up." 先听，不要先卖。用沉默让对方打开。

### Falsify，不要 validate
Judd Antin: "We don't validate, we falsify. We look to be wrong. Many PMs want to be right—they do user-centered performance, not real research." 真研究是在寻找自己错在哪里，而不是表演 user-centered。

### 永远不要问用户想让你构建什么
Judd Antin: "A researcher who asks customers what they want is a bad researcher. Focus on understanding behaviors and problems—not having users design your solution." 聚焦行为和问题，不要让用户替你设计 solution。

### 追问 emotion
Nan Yu: "My goal is to feel bad the same way customers feel bad. Dig past the feature request to find the underlying negative emotion motivating it." 穿过 feature request，找到背后的负面情绪。

### 放下 discussion guide
Bob Moesta: "Not having a script drives people crazy, but rigid guides prevent you from following meaningful threads. Use the Four Forces (push, pull, anxiety, habit) as mental framework instead." 用 Four Forces 做心智框架，而不是被僵硬脚本困住。

### 样本量要 right-size
Shaun Clowes: "Between 7-14 interviews, you stop learning new things. Less than 7, not enough data. More than 14, diminishing returns." 7-14 次访谈通常能覆盖主要模式。

### 预期 90% 拒绝
Gustaf Alstromer: "90% of people aren't early adopters. You need to reach 10 to find 1. Rejection isn't failure—it's filtering for the right users." 拒绝不是失败，而是在过滤正确用户。

### 获得 direct exposure
Marty Cagan: "I wasn't allowed to make product decisions until I'd visited 30 customers. Those visits changed my life—I thought I knew our customers and I really didn't." PM 和 designers 需要直接见到客户，而不只是读报告。

### 以极快速度回应
Jeff Weinstein: "When a customer goes out of their way to share a problem, that's a gift. I'll leave a meeting to reply. Be 'text message friendly' with 5-10 power users." 当客户主动分享问题时，那是一份礼物。

### 访谈 non-users
Mihika Kapoor: "The most insightful conversations are with non-users. Ask why they're not using your product—you'll find perception gaps users can't see." Non-users 往往能揭示 users 看不到的 perception gaps。

### 测试 willingness to pay
Jeff Weinstein: "Have them send you a $1 invoice right now. The gap between 'willingness to pay' and actually paying is massive. This tests real commitment." "愿意付费" 和真的付款之间差距巨大。

### 与 lighthouse users 共创
Tanguy Crusson: "Work with 10 'lighthouse' users over months. Put them in Slack with your team. Involve engineers directly so they build empathy." 让 engineers 直接参与，建立 empathy。

## 帮助用户的问题

- "你希望从这些 interviews 里学到什么？"
- "你访谈的是已经尝试解决这个问题的人吗？"
- "你如何招募 participants？"
- "你的 opening question 是什么？（确保它要求讲 story，而不是 opinion）"
- "你如何避免 leading questions？"
- "你会如何处理 findings？"

## 需要提醒的常见错误

- **Leading questions** - "Don't you think X would be better?" 只会得到附和
- **询问 hypotheticals** - "Would you use this?" 没意义；行为才重要
- **研究中 pitching** - 你是去学习，不是去销售
- **访谈太少** - 2 次不够；目标是 7-14 次
- **委托 observation** - PMs 和 designers 必须在现场，而不只是读 reports
