## Annie Duke
*Annie Duke*

> "So a pre-mortem, it's great only if you set up kill criteria. Commit to actions that you're going to take if you see those signals."

**洞察：** Pre-mortem 只有在产出 'kill criteria' 时才有效，也就是会触发 pivot 或 shutdown 的预设 signals。

**战术建议：**
- 在 pre-mortem 中识别 project 正在失败的 early signals。
- 如果这些 signals 出现，提前承诺采取 specific actions（例如 kill the project）。

*时间戳：00:00:28*

---

> "What a pre-mortem allows you to do is to set up kill criteria. So kill criteria are just a set of signals that you might see that would tell you that it's time to pivot or stop because once we actually launch something, we're very, very slow to decide to quit."

**洞察：** Pre-mortems 通过在对 project 产生 emotional attachment 前设立 objective exit points，抵消 'sunk cost' bias。

**战术建议：**
- 问 team：'Imagine it is six months from now and the project has failed. What were the early signals?'
- 基于这些 signals 创建 'kill criteria' list（例如无法让 decision-maker 进入房间）。

*时间戳：01:06:18*


## Ben Williams
*Ben Williams*

> "We have these team level impact and learnings reviews... The teams continuously document any learnings from data exploration, from experimentation, from user research and so on. They document that in their weekly impact and learnings document... Most of it is spent discussing learnings that have been documented, their implications, how they can be leveraged in follow up work."

**洞察：** 将 'learnings review' ceremony 制度化，能确保 experiment data 真正被用于 future decisions。

**战术建议：**
- 每周举行聚焦 insights 而不是 status updates 的 'Impact and Learnings' reviews
- 在 entire company 中 socialize learnings，提升其他 teams

*时间戳：01:07:00*


## Carole Robin
*Carole Robin*

> "The acronym is A-F-O-G, another F-ing... Another Fucking Opportunity for Growth. Every student who ever took a class from me... knows that acronym because my question, when something has gone wrong or a person has experienced a failure, my first question is always, so what did you learn?"

**洞察：** 把 failure reframed as learning opportunity，对 long-term growth 和 resilience 至关重要。

**战术建议：**
- 发生 failure 时，立刻问：'What is the lesson here?'
- 在 painful setbacks 中使用 AFOG acronym 保持 perspective。

*时间戳：01:17:02*


## Christina Wodtke
*Christina Wodtke*

> "What matters is, why 80%? Really focus on the learning... It's all about the retrospectives. Make sure your grading is secondary to retrospective, is the biggest thing I would say, because that's what's going to be valuable."

**洞察：** Grading OKRs 的价值在于 retrospective analysis：为什么 goal 达成或没达成，而不是数字本身。

**战术建议：**
- 优先关注 grade 背后的 'why'，而不是 numerical score 的 precision。
- 使用 end-of-quarter retrospective 识别阻碍达到 100% 的 systemic blockers。

*时间戳：00:53:20*


## Eeke de Milliano
*Eeke de Milliano*

> "Instead of calling something a postmortem, call it a retrospective, so that it's a positive thing. Like, 'Hey, we're learning from this thing.'"

**洞察：** 将 post-mortems reframed as retrospectives，有助于 normalize failure，并让 team 聚焦 learning 而不是 blame。

**战术建议：**
- 将 'post-mortems' 重命名为 'retrospectives'
- 公开讨论 failure，降低对 taking big swings 的恐惧

*时间戳：00:22:14*

---

> "Have people talk about the failure in these sort of public forums, where usually you talk about the successes. So have someone actually write a note that's like, 'Hey, here are all my learnings from this thing that we did.' And send it to the org."

**洞察：** 公开 celebrating failure 中的 learnings，会强化 innovation 和 psychological safety 的 culture。

**战术建议：**
- 鼓励 teams 向 entire company 发送关于 failed projects 的 'learning notes'
- 让 teams 在 all-hands meetings 中展示 failures 和 lessons learned

*时间戳：00:28:18*

## Maggie Crowley
*Maggie Crowley*

> "I remember... we decided we needed to do a rewrite, red flag number one... It didn't take six months, it took two and a half years... We skipped discovery. We didn't really write a one-pager, we just went for it."

**洞察：** Failed projects 往往源于跳过 discovery，以及低估 technical rewrites 的 complexity。

**战术建议：**
- 避免对 core products 做 'side-by-side' rewrites
- 对 large technical projects 永远不要跳过 discovery 或 requirements gathering
- 当 projects 失控时，诚实面对 'sunk cost fallacy'

*时间戳：00:56:47*


## Melissa Perri
*Melissa*

> "I think the biggest message I can tell to anybody learning, is really, sit down, do a retrospective with yourself, and say, is this helping me get better at being a product manager? And if it's not, change it."

**洞察：** Product management 中的 personal growth，需要把 retrospective framework 应用于自己的 career 和 learning habits。

**战术建议：**
- 定期 audit 自己的 frameworks 'toolbox'，丢弃不再服务于 specific context 的内容。

## Mike Krieger
*Mike Krieger*

> "The confluence of those three things [mobile web deterioration, lack of viral spread, remote work]... we entered I guess 2024 and said, 'Look, there is a company to be built in the space. I'm not sure where the people would've built it. This concurrent incarnation we love, but it's not growing.'"

**洞察：** Shutting down product 需要识别 system 中何时没有 'energy'，并有 intellectual honesty 尽早喊停。

**战术建议：**
- 评估 input（effort）到 output（growth）的 ratio 是否 fundamentally broken。
- 评估 failure 时，同时考虑 platform deterioration（例如 mobile web）等 external factors，以及 team distribution 等 internal factors。

*时间戳：00:38:39*


## Ronny Kohavi
*Ronny Kohavi*

> "To me, a surprising experiment is one where the estimated result beforehand and the actual result differ by a lot. ... So we focused not just on the winners, but also surprising losers, things that people thought would be a no-brainer to run. And then for some reason, it was very negative. And sometimes, it's that negative that gives you insight."

**洞察：** Retrospectives 应聚焦 'surprising' results，也就是 prediction 和 reality 之间 delta 最大的地方，以最大化 organizational learning。

**战术建议：**
- 每季度专门开会 review 最 surprising 的 experiment results
- 分析 'surprising losers'，揭示 battery life impact 或 user friction 等 hidden factors

*时间戳：00:17:53*


## Shreyas Doshi
*Shreyas Doshi*

> "The idea is simple, which is when you are working on an important project or initiative, you get together with your team early on in the products or the projects' life to see in advance what could go wrong. And the way I describe a pre-mortem is that if you do a pre-mortem right, you will not have to do an ugly post-mortem."

**洞察：** Pre-mortems 使用 hypothetical failure state 创造 psychological safety，让 team members 能说出原本会 withheld 的 concerns。

**战术建议：**
- 从这个 prompt 开始：'Imagine this project has failed six months from now. What went wrong?'
- 将 risks 分类为 'Tigers'（lethal threats）、'Paper Tigers'（perceived threats）和 'Elephants'（unspoken issues）。
- 纳入 engineering、sales、support 和 marketing 的 cross-functional members。

*时间戳：00:24:38*
