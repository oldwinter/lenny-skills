# Written Communication

使用来自 38 位产品领导者的框架和洞察，帮助用户更有效地进行 written communication。

## 如何提供帮助

当用户请求 written communication 相关帮助时：

1. **澄清 purpose** - 询问他们希望 readers 做什么 action 或产生什么 outcome
2. **应用 pyramid principle** - 指导他们先给 conclusion，而不是铺垫到最后
3. **Ruthlessly cut** - 帮助他们删掉 unnecessary context，更快进入重点
4. **测试 simplicity** - 确保不熟悉 topic 的人也能理解 core message

## 核心原则

### Focus on the how, not the what
Wes Kao: "Most writers spend too much time on the what and the why and not enough time on how. Most readers already agree with the general premise of what you're saying." 最小化 context 和 justification；关注 specific steps、nuances 和 examples。

### Writing clarifies thinking
Julie Zhuo: "I approach my writing as letters to myself. This is the framework, this is the advice I need to give myself. It was hugely helpful for clarifying my train of thought." 把 writing 当作组织 scattered thoughts、迫使 clarity 的 tool。

### Writing is clarity at scale
Kevin Yien: "Writing is clarity at scale. A key component to a PM's job is creating clarity both internally and externally." 用 customer 的 voice 写作，展示 deep understanding。

### 每个 project 都需要 one canonical doc
Naomi Gleit: "There needs to be one canonical doc. Everyone should know exactly where it is. It links to all other docs." 为每个 project 创建 single source of truth。

### Communication is the job
Boz: "Communication is the job. Leadership is exclusively done through the creation of artifacts or verbalizations that affect other humans." 对 message 是否被成功接收承担 extreme ownership。

### 从最高 tension 前一刻开始
Wes Kao: "Don't start talking about going to REI to buy a Patagonia jacket. Start right before your friend left a Clif Bar out and you almost got mauled by a bear." Cut preamble，从 highest tension 或 relevance 的地方开始。

### Conclusion first, always
Wes Kao: "In business, start with your conclusion, then here's why. Not here's all my thinking, here's all my data points, and then my conclusion at the end." 把 recommendation 放在最顶部。

### Write what you repeat
Deb Liu: "Write what you repeat. If you say something more than once, write it down. Then the next time someone asks, you can just hand them." 把 recurring advice document 下来，用来 scale your influence。

### Concision 是 density，不只是 brevity
Wes Kao: "Being concise is not about absolute word count, it's about economy of words. You can have a 300 word memo that's meandering and a thousand word memo that is tight and concise." 关注 insight density，而不只是把内容变短。

### 跨 modalities 重复
Boz: "I will give an all-hands and then write a post with the content of the all-hands, because different people respond differently to these modalities." 使用 multiple channels and formats，确保 messages 被吸收。

### 使用 numbered lists 便于 reference
Naomi Gleit: "I never use bulleted lists because you can never refer to a bullet. I always use numbered lists because you can say 'as referenced in number two.'" 让 discussions 中的 precise referencing 成为可能。

### Full sentences 暴露 logic gaps
Wes Kao: "I dislike when people overuse bullets and sentence fragments when they should use complete sentences that show the connected tissue between ideas." 把 bullet fragments 变成 full sentences，用来测试 idea 是否 fully thought out。

## 帮助用户的问题

- "你希望 reader 读完后做什么？"
- "你的 conclusion 是什么？能否在顶部用一句话说明？"
- "前两段真的必要吗，还是 preamble？"
- "不熟悉这个 topic 的人能否理解 core point？"
- "这是不是你已经解释过多次、应该 document 的东西？"
- "你是否大声读过它，检查听起来是否像 natural conversation？"

## 需要提醒的常见错误

- **Building up to the conclusion** - Business writing 应该先给 answer，而不是 build suspense
- **Too much context** - Cut the backstory；从 relevance point 开始
- **Bullet fragments hiding weak logic** - Full sentences 会迫使 clearer thinking
- **Single-channel communication** - Important messages 需要 multiple modalities
- **Assuming one communication is enough** - Messages 需要跨 formats 重复
