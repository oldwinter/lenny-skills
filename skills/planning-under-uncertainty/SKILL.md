# Planning Under Uncertainty

使用来自 44 位产品领导者的 adaptive planning frameworks，帮助用户在未来不清晰时推进 product planning。

## 如何提供帮助

当用户请求 planning under uncertainty 相关帮助时：

1. **理解 uncertainty 类型** - 询问 ambiguity 来自哪里：technical unknowns、market volatility、AI/ML unpredictability，还是 organizational change
2. **评估 planning horizon** - 判断他们需要 short-term execution tactics，还是 long-term strategic flexibility
3. **让 framework 匹配 context** - 根据 uncertainty profile 推荐合适 planning approaches
4. **内置 adaptation mechanisms** - 帮助创建用于 pivoting 的 checkpoints 和 decision criteria

## 核心原则

### 拥抱 optionality，而不是 prediction
Amjad Masad: "Being agile, not being stuck with roadmaps, being able to just say, oh, we're just going to switch priorities right away, is going to be super important." 在 AI 这类快速变化环境中，保持在新 capabilities 出现时 pivot 的灵活性，而不是承诺僵硬长期 plans。

### 为 chaos 构建 buffers
Upasna Gautam: "Any time we're planning we build in buffers for all of that chaos that's happening on a daily basis." 在混乱环境中，planning 必须包含明确 time buffers 和 contingency plans；范围可按 scope 从几天到几个月不等。

### 把 data 当 compass，不是 GPS
Shaun Clowes: "Data is more like a compass than a GPS. If you look at data as a way of giving you the answer, you're always wrong." 用 data validate 或 invalidate intuition，而不是等它告诉你具体该做什么。

### 重视 learning 胜过 winning
Ramesh Johari: "Experimentation was never historically in science about winners and losers... Experimentation is always very hypothesis driven. It's about, what are you learning?" 健康 experimentation culture 重视从 "failed" risky bets 中学习，而不是只追求安全、incremental "wins"。

### 建立 reproducible testing processes
Nikita Bier: "Develop a reproducible testing process, and that will actually influence the probability of your success more than anything." 在不确定市场中，成功更多取决于 testing process 的质量和速度，而不是 initial idea。

### 危机中先 diagnose，再 action
Alex Hardimen: "There's this incredible humility that was needed to really understand and first diagnose what was actually happening on the platform." 管理 crisis 需要 "wartime" humility，在尝试 solution 前准确诊断问题。

### 创建 decision triggers，而不是 fixed plans
Eric Ries: "Give yourself a fixed period of time to take some decisive action and see if it feels better." 在 plans 中设置 checkpoints，根据学到的东西 reassess，而不只是根据 calendar dates。

### 区分 reversible 和 irreversible decisions
Claire Hughes Johnson: "Type one, type two decisions. Is it high impact? Is it irreversible? Is it not?" 对 one-way doors 花更多时间；对可调整的 reversible decisions 快速行动。

## 帮助用户的问题

- "你的当前 plan 要成立，需要哪些事情为真？其中哪些 assumptions 你最没信心？"
- "如果这件事花两倍预期时间，你会怎么做？如果只花一半时间呢？"
- "为了验证 core hypothesis，你能 ship 的最小东西是什么？"
- "这是 one-way door 还是 two-way door decision？"
- "哪些 signals 会告诉你应该 pivot 或 kill 这个 initiative？"
- "你为 unexpected chaos 留了多少 buffer？"

## 需要提醒的常见错误

- **Over-planning** - 创建详细长期 roadmaps，制造 false confidence，并抵抗必要 pivots
- **Analysis paralysis** - 等待完美信息，而不是在 70% confidence 时做决策
- **忽视 leading indicators** - 没有追踪能更早说明方向是否正确的 intermediate signals
- **只按 outcomes 判断 experiments** - 不重视测试重要 hypotheses 的 "failed" experiments 所带来的 learning
- **Planning theater** - 在无法减少真实 uncertainty 的 documents 和 processes 上花过多时间
