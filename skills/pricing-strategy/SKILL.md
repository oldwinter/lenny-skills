# Pricing Strategy

使用来自 46 位产品领导者的框架，帮助用户设计并优化 pricing strategies。

## 如何提供帮助

当用户请求 pricing 相关帮助时：

1. **理解 business model** - 询问当前 monetization approach 和 target customer segments
2. **评估 value delivery** - 判断他们正在为谁创造什么 value
3. **识别 constraints** - 理解 technical、market 和 competitive pricing pressures
4. **为 iteration 设计** - 帮助创建可随着学习而演进的 pricing

## 核心原则

### Price 是 value 的衡量
Madhavan Ramanujam: "When we think about price, we think about it as a measure. Like liter is a measure of volume, price is a measure of value. And when you think of it this way, it really stands for, do people actually want your product?" 用 willingness to pay（WTP）作为 product value proxy，并在 development cycle 早期进行 WTP conversations。

### Never set it and forget it
Naomi Ionita: "Do not set it and forget it. I see companies do this, where they labor over designs and features... And then pricing's sort of plucked out of thin air, and then they don't revisit it." 把 pricing 当作 product roadmap 的 living part，随着 new value 增加，每 6-12 个月重新审视。

### Self-serve 有 ceiling
Elena Verna: "Self-serve monetization has a cap of about $10,000. That's just how much we're able to process on the credit cards before they start getting flagged and declined." 对超过 $10,000-$15,000 的 contracts，因 technical payment limits 和 individual spending authority，需要转向 sales-led motions。

### 在 free experience 中 sample premium features
Albert Cheng: "What if we actually sampled a number of different paid suggestions and interspersed them to free users across their writing? All of a sudden, people were seeing Grammarly as a much more powerful tool." 在 free experience 中穿插 paid features，比把它们全部藏在 hard paywall 后更利于 conversion。

### 降低 early friction 来解锁 success
Archie Abrams: "When you can lower the barriers to monetary friction in some form... you can actually causally change your ability to become successful, because I've given you a little bit more time to try that idea." 降低 early monetary friction 可以通过延长用户寻找 value 的 "runway"，因果性提升 user success。

### 用 willingness to pay 排 roadmap
Madhavan Ramanujam: "You cannot prioritize a product roadmap without having a willingness to pay conversation. If you're just prioritizing based on what you think or what you feel or technical resources, you're getting it wrong." 优先构建驱动 80% willingness to pay 的 20% features。

### Pricing changes 是 two-way doors
Eeke de Milliano: "Move faster on reversible decisions like pricing by grandfathering existing users." 大多数 pricing changes 可以 reversed 或 adjusted，尤其当你通过 grandfathering 保护 existing customers 时。

## 帮助用户的问题

- "如果 customers 今天必须付费，他们会为这个付多少？你直接问过吗？"
- "你的 product 创造什么 value？哪个 customer segment 获得的 value 最高？"
- "你上一次 revisit pricing 是什么时候？从那以后 product 发生了什么变化？"
- "是什么阻止你 charge more？Competition、value delivery，还是 assumption？"
- "Free users 今天如何体验 paid features 的 value？"
- "在哪个 price point 上，sales motion 需要从 self-serve 转为 sales-assisted？"

## 需要提醒的常见错误

- **Pricing as afterthought** - 基于 gut feel 而不是 willingness to pay research 定价
- **Never iterating** - 带着一个 price launch 后，随着 product 演进也不再 revisiting
- **Hard paywalls on premium** - 隐藏所有 premium value，而不是在 free experience 中 sample
- **忽视 self-serve ceiling** - 试图通过 credit card checkout close $50K deals
- **Racing to the bottom** - 在 price 上竞争，而不是在 value 上 differentiation
