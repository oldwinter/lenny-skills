# Running Effective 1:1s

使用来自 7 位产品领导者的框架，帮助用户开展有效 one-on-one meetings。

## 如何提供帮助

当用户请求 1:1s 相关帮助时：

1. **理解关系** - 询问他们的 role、report 的 tenure 和当前 dynamics
2. **识别 purpose** - 判断 1:1 需要聚焦 tactical work、career development，还是 emotional support
3. **建议 structure** - 根据情况推荐合适 formats
4. **辅导 coaching** - 帮助他们从 advising 转向 empowering reports

## 核心原则

### Coach, don't advise
Rachel Lockett: "Great leaders know that when you try to advise and have the answer all the time, you're not actually equipping your team to go solve the hard problems. You're training your team to come to you with all of the hard problems." 当 report 带来 hard problem 时，把能量转向 curiosity。避免立即给答案的冲动。

### 为深度 career conversations 留时间
Kim Scott: "I would have three separate 45 minute conversations, so one about their past, one about their future, and one about their present to create a plan." 进行 "Life Story" conversation 理解 motivations，进行 "Future Dreams" conversation 识别 long-term visions，再创建 "Career Action Plan" 把当前 skill-building 连接到这些 dreams。

### 关注 recovery 和 joy
Hilary Gridley: "In my one on ones... I'm asking them, 'What do you do for joy? Are you doing something every single day that's bringing you joy in your life?' And if they say no, I'm like, 'That's a problem.'" 直接询问 reports 工作之外什么给他们 joy。把缺少 daily joy 当作需要计划处理的 performance risk。

### 考虑减少 standing 1:1s
Howie Liu: "I actually cut my one-on-one roster by default... Just having more standing one-on-ones actually precludes me from engaging in more timely topics." 考虑 barbell approach：高质量、低频关系 catch-ups（例如 monthly walks）加上按需安排的 urgent topical meetings。

### 危机后用 1:1 做 emotional processing
Matt Mochary: "With each and every person on the stay team, you have a one-on-one with their manager for one hour and all the manager does is say, 'I'd like to know your thoughts and feelings,' and the person shares and then all the manager does is make them feel heard." Post-crisis 1:1s 应完全聚焦 active listening 和 emotional processing。

### 1:1 属于 report
Report 应 own agenda。作为 manager，你在这里是支持他们成功，而不是获取 status updates。

## 帮助用户的问题

- "当 report 带着问题来找你时，你倾向于给答案，还是问问题？"
- "你上一次做深度 career development conversation（不是 performance review）是什么时候？"
- "你如何知道 report 在工作外恢复得好不好？"
- "你的 report owns 1:1 agenda，还是由你驱动？"
- "你的 1:1s 是 tactical 还是 strategic？正确 balance 是什么？"

## 需要提醒的常见错误

- **Status update theater** - 用 1:1 做本可以 async 的 updates
- **总是在 advising** - 替 report 解决问题，而不是 coaching 他们解决问题
- **跳过 career conversations** - 只讨论 immediate work，从不讨论 long-term development
- **忽视 wellbeing** - 没注意 report 正在 burnout
- **Manager-driven agenda** - Manager 决定 topics，而不是 report own 这段时间
