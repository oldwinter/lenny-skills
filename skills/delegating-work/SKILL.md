# Delegating Work

使用来自 38 位产品领导者的框架，帮助用户有效授权；这些领导者曾扩展过自己和组织的杠杆。

## 如何提供帮助

当用户请求 delegation 相关帮助时：

1. **诊断 blocker** - 判断他们紧抓任务是因为 identity、control，还是 lack of trust
2. **识别可授权内容** - 使用 energy audits 和 Eisenhower Matrix 找到候选任务
3. **让 delegation style 匹配 context** - 帮助他们在 autonomy 和 involvement 之间校准
4. **为成功设计** - 用 context、parameters 和 feedback loops 设置好 delegation

## 核心原则

### Context，不是 control
Ray Cao: "The number one thing is context, not control. That's the reason why we're always encouraging people to see themselves as a business owner." 提供所有必要信息，让员工能自己 'connect the dots'，而不是等待指令。

### 拒绝替别人裁决
Boz: "One of the most powerful things we do is refuse to rule. Someone will bring me a thing. I'll be like, 'Nope, but look, I think you've got it.'" 当团队正在挣扎于 "正确" 问题时，有意后退一步来建立 autonomy。

### Give away your Legos
Cam Adams: "Finding joy in the other things of building a team, passing on your experience, helping other people do great writing or great product building is really what giving away your Lego is about." 从帮助他人成功中获得满足，而不是为了个人 identity 抓住任务不放。

### 深入细节不等于 micromanagement
Brian Chesky: "There's a difference between micromanagement, which is like telling people exactly what to do, and being in the details. If you don't know the details, how do you know people are doing a good job?" 深入参与能保障质量；micromanagement 是规定每个动作。

### 不要授权掉你的 superpower
Brian Chesky (citing Ben Horowitz): "A lot of people tell product led founders to step away and delegate their product to other people, but suddenly they've delegated away the thing they're best at. The thing that is hardest for them to replace." 保留你核心能力中的直接参与，因为那往往最难替代。

### 从 doer 转向 editor
Fareed Mosavat: "You have to shift from, 'My job is to do the work,' to, 'My job is to make the work better. My job is to plus the work, to review the work.'" 走向 management 意味着成为 editor，而不只是 delegator。

### 使用 selective micromanagement
Ravi Mehta: "If you don't feel confident in the direction that your team is moving, the right answer is not to be hands-off. The right answer is to micromanage, but do it in a very tactical and a very temporary way." 知道什么时候该深入介入，什么时候该退回来。

### 六个月 calibration test
Peter Deng: "In 6 months, if I'm telling you what to do, I've hired the wrong person. The meta goal becomes, are we calibrating enough? Are we getting into a spot where you're the one telling me what needs to be done?" 有效 delegation 意味着六个月内由 direct report 来驱动工作。

## 帮助用户的问题

- "哪些任务是你因为 identity 而不是 necessity 抓着不放？"
- "如果做 energy audit，哪些 calendar items 会是 red（draining）？"
- "这件事需要做吗？别人能做吗？还是你能改变 methodology？"
- "你是在提供 context 和 parameters，还是规定每一步？"
- "六个月后，是这个人告诉你该做什么，还是仍在等你指令？"

## 需要提醒的常见错误

- **授权掉你的 superpower** - 把最难替代的核心能力交出去
- **All-or-nothing thinking** - 以为只能 micromanage 或彻底 hands-off
- **没有 context 就 delegation** - 没给成功所需信息，却期待结果
- **不愿 teach** - 前期不投入时间训练别人，然后自己被淹没
- **早期过度 delegation** - 太早招聘 senior executives，而 founders 本应继续深入细节
