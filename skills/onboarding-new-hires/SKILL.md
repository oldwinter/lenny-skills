# Onboarding New Hires

使用来自 14 位产品领导者的方法，帮助用户有效 onboarding 新团队成员。

## 如何提供帮助

当用户请求 onboarding 相关帮助时：

1. **理解上下文** - 询问他们是在 onboarding 别人还是自己 being onboarded、seniority level，以及这是 new role 还是 backfill
2. **设计结构** - 帮助创建包含清晰 milestones 和 success criteria 的 30-60-90 day plan
3. **优先 belonging 和 learning** - 确保 plan 包含 social integration，而不只是 tasks
4. **设计 early wins** - 识别让新员工快速展示 value 的机会

## 核心原则

### 先 listen，再 act：治疗前先诊断
Deb Liu: "The first 90 days are focused on listening and learning first, then doing. Conduct a 'listening tour' in the first 30 days, meeting as many stakeholders as possible." 把 findings 总结成 "state of the union"，让团队知道他们被听见了。30-60 天对齐 vision，到第 90 天进入 full execution。

### 定义 90 天、1 年和 2 年的 success
Lauren Ipsen: "The 90-day plan is overused but necessary, and that's just the tip of the iceberg. What should this person be doing a year from now? Two years from now?" 在开始招聘前，先定义每个 milestone 上 "crushing it" 是什么样。

### 通过 pairing 立即创造 belonging
Heidi Helfand: "Help them feel a sense of belonging by not having their first day be them sitting alone. Have someone be a 'first pair.'" 确保新员工第一天不是独自工作。分配 buddy 立即与他们协作。鼓励分享 personal stories 来建立 connection。

### 第一个 Friday 前教会 golden rituals
Shishir Mehrotra: "Great companies have a very small list of golden rituals. Every employee knows them by their first Friday." 把核心 company rituals 纳入 onboarding curriculum。这些 rituals 应该被命名、被知道、被模板化。

### 为 visibility 设计 early wins
Melissa Tan: "How do I help them secure early wins? I'll suggest 'You should do this presentation - it's a great way to get visibility early.'" 为新员工识别 low-hanging fruit projects。在最初几周创造向 leadership 展示的机会。

### 公开记录你的 philosophy
Brandon Chu: "People joining my team already knew how I thought because they'd Google me. It passively onboarded a lot of the PMs." 写下你的 management 和 product philosophy。新员工可以在第一天前就 ramp up。

### 做一次 "relationship design" conversation
Kenneth Berger: "For maybe your first 10-20 employees, have a relationship design conversation when they're hired." 结合具体 roles 和 personalities，一起 figuring out 最佳合作方式。这会预防后续 friction 和 "out of integrity" behavior。

### Hiring 时不要 overpromise
Molly Graham: "Do not promise things you can't control. Be honest about the ambiguity and chaos of a scaling environment." 避免承诺你无法保证的 stability、specific titles 或 career paths。Overpromises 会变成 "letter bombs"，爆炸时打击 high performers。

### 用清晰 cadences 加速 ownership
Timothy Davis: "I want to try and make onboarding 30-45 days by giving them responsibility early. Here's everything we did, here's your responsibilities, go." 使用 ops cadence spreadsheet 定义 task frequency。在最初几周就分配 campaign 或 project ownership。

### 用 AI 降低 knowledge access friction
Scott Wu: "New engineers can ask Devin questions about the codebase. It's nice to be able to ask without social pressure." AI tools 可以帮助新员工探索 internal systems，并在没有社交压力的情况下问 "dumb questions"。

## 帮助用户的问题

- "对这个人来说，30、60、90 天的 success 分别是什么样？"
- "第一天谁是他们的 'first pair' 或 buddy？"
- "他们第一个 Friday 前需要知道哪些 company golden rituals？"
- "我们能设计哪个 early win 来给他们 visibility 和 credibility？"
- "你是否把自己的 philosophy 写在某处，让他们入职前就能读？"
- "Hiring 时你承诺了什么，现在需要兑现？"

## 需要提醒的常见错误

- **没有 structured 90-day plan** - 新员工需要清晰 milestones。"Figure it out" 不是 onboarding
- **第一天让他们独处** - Isolation 会破坏 belonging。从一开始就分配 pair
- **全是 tasks，没有 relationships** - Onboarding 是 social，不只是 functional。优先 introductions 和 connection
- **Ownership 太晚** - 不要几个月后才给 responsibility。用几周时间找到 assign ownership 的方式
- **Hiring 时 over-promising** - 对 chaos 和 ambiguity 保持诚实。破碎承诺会打击士气
