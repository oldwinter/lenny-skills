# Setting OKRs & Goals

使用来自 55 位产品领导者的框架和洞察，帮助用户创建有效 objectives 和 key results。

## 如何提供帮助

当用户请求 OKRs 或 goal setting 相关帮助时：

1. **理解 context** - 询问 company stage、team size，以及是否已有 goals 或从零开始
2. **澄清 level** - 判断这些是 company、department、team 还是 individual goals
3. **识别 strategy** - 确保他们先有清晰 strategy 再设定 goals（goals 应该是 strategy 的终点，而不是起点）
4. **指导 structure** - 帮助他们写出 inspiring objectives 和 measurable key results，并避开常见陷阱

## 核心原则

### Goals 应该离 company goals 只有一步
Matt LeMay: "No more than one step away from company goals. Don't let it get cascaded into oblivion." 确保 team goals 直接围绕 primary company objective 运转，而不是被埋在多层 organizational cascading 下面。

### Build systems, not just goals
Lane Shackleton: "Instead of being obsessed with the goal, be obsessed with the system that gets you there." 用 recurring、'default-on' systems 替代一次性 OKR targets，比如 standing customer meetings，用来构建 long-term instincts。

### OKRs 连接 strategy 和 execution
Christina Wodtke: "The main benefit is that there's a lot of concrete action through an OKR that you don't always get from strategy." 使用 OKRs 把 abstract strategy 转化为 concrete quarterly numbers，同时通过 end-of-quarter grading 创建 learning cycle。

### 用三个 key results 做 triangulate
Christina Wodtke: "I like three. Something that's really hardcore numbers, something that's a little squishier like quality, and something that involves a dollar sign." 平衡 quantitative、qualitative 和 financial metrics，形成完整 success view。

### 使用 absolute numbers，而不是 ratios
Archie Abrams: "Ratios and percentages are dangerous metrics because they can be 'gamed' by reducing the denominator." 把 goals 从 'conversion rate' 转为 'absolute number of users reaching a specific state'，避免 gaming。

### Ambitious goals 会迫使 new thinking
Daniel Lereya: "Put ambitious goals, it'll make you think differently. We really love to do it even when we don't know it's possible." 设定无法只靠 'working harder' 实现的 goals，迫使团队 'working smarter' 并进行 fundamental rethinks。

### 把 strategy 和 OKR discussions 分开
Lane Shackleton: "OKRs are not actually strategy. It's critical to disconnect strategy discussions from OKR discussions." 在 OKR planning 前创建独立 strategy ritual，确保 'why' 不会迷失在 'what' 里。

### 限制 planning overhead
Lane Shackleton: "The 10% planning rule - ensure you're not planning for more than 10% of that execution period." 如果是为一个 quarter 做 planning，planning process 最多花大约一周。

### 关注 outcomes，而不是 outputs
Marty Cagan: "In a real product team, you celebrate when you actually solve the problem. That's why we say product teams are about outcomes, not output." 用 problem resolution 定义 success，而不是 feature shipment。

### Goals 是 communication tools
Molly Graham: "No company needs more than three company goals. The point is to help people know what the most important things are." 保持 goals 简单，确保冲突时有一个会胜出，并给每个 goal 精确分配一个 owner。

## 帮助用户的问题

- "你们公司现在的 primary goal 是什么，这个 team goal 如何与它连接？"
- "你如何知道自己成功了？哪个 specific number 会改变？"
- "这个 objective 是否足够 inspiring，能激励团队穿过 obstacles？"
- "你的 key results 是 outcomes（如何知道成功了），还是 tasks（to-do list）？"
- "你可以 build 什么 system，让这个 goal 的 progress 变成 'default-on'？"
- "如果你达成了这个 goal，但伤害了 user experience，它仍然算成功吗？"

## 需要提醒的常见错误

- **Key results that are tasks** - Key results 应该是 measurable outcomes，而不是待完成 activities 的 checklist
- **Too many goals** - Companies 不需要超过三个 goals；更多会稀释 focus 并阻碍 trade-offs
- **Vague objectives** - Objectives 应该让人愿意起床投入，而不是 generic corporate language
- **Goals set before strategy** - 如果没有真正 strategy，OKRs 会变成 false precision 的无意义 exercise
- **Over-indexing on a single metric** - 这会激励保留对 customers 不友好、但能推动数字的 features
