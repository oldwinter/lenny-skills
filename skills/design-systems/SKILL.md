# Design Systems

使用来自 4 位产品领导者的框架，帮助用户构建并扩展 design systems；他们曾在 Figma、Airbnb 等公司建设 design systems。

## 如何提供帮助

当用户请求 design systems 相关帮助时：

1. **评估需求** - 判断他们需要 consistency、speed，还是两者都需要，以及当前阶段是否适合投资 design system
2. **定义 scope** - 澄清他们需要 component library、design tokens、documentation，还是三者都需要
3. **为 adoption 设计** - 帮助他们把 system 做到足够易用，让 non-designers 也能正确使用
4. **规划 evolution** - 指导他们如何长期维护和演进 system

## 核心原则

### 分离 concept 和 production
Bob Baxley: "Once we locked down on the block frames, we could send it to an agency and they could do the full high-res comps in a day, because they knew exactly what they were doing." 用 low-fidelity 'block brain diagrams' 锁定概念逻辑，再用 design system 快速产出 high-fidelity output。

### Design systems 推动 enterprise expansion
Claire Butler: "Design systems are one of the main reasons you upgrade from pro to org or enterprise. That became just the key thing we leaned in on." Design systems practitioners 是组织规模化中的关键 internal champions。

### Assets 应该自带使用教学
Jessica Hische: "My goal always when designing a logo is to design a logo that's so easy to use that you don't have to be an extremely skilled designer to design well with it." 设计 assets 时，让其内在结构能教会用户如何应用；优先易用性，而不是复杂度。

### Flat design 正在演进
Brian Chesky: "I think flat design is over or ending. We're going to move back into a world with color, texture, dimensionality, more haptic feedback." Interface design 正从 flat aesthetics 转向更有维度、更触觉化、AI-enhanced 的体验。

## 帮助用户的问题

- "你今天面对的最大 inconsistency problem 是什么？"
- "谁会使用这个 design system：只有 designers，还是 engineers 也会用？"
- "你如何衡量 design system 的 adoption 和 success？"
- "你是否有资源长期维护和演进这个 system？"
- "你可以先 ship 的 smallest viable version 是什么？"

## 需要提醒的常见错误

- **过早构建** - 还没有足够 patterns 可 systematize，就开始创建 design system
- **Over-engineering** - 构建只有 expert designers 才能正确使用的复杂系统
- **没有 ownership** - 创建 design system，却没有 dedicated resources 来维护
- **忽视 adoption** - 构建漂亮但没人实际使用的系统
- **Static systems** - 把 design system 当作已经 "done"，而不是持续演进
