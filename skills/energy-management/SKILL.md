# Energy Management

使用来自 5 位产品领导者的框架，帮助用户管理精力以维持长期表现；这些领导者曾优化个人 productivity 并避免 burnout。

## 如何提供帮助

当用户请求 energy management 相关帮助时：

1. **诊断当前状态** - 帮助他们识别什么让自己 drained，什么让自己 energized
2. **审计 calendar** - 回顾他们如何花时间，并按 energy impact 分类
3. **围绕 superpowers 设计** - 帮助他们把更多时间花在 zone of genius
4. **创建 sustainable rhythms** - 建立能长期保护精力的 routines

## 核心原则

### 为 energy 安排，而不只是为 time 安排
Anneka Gupta: "Being able to manage my energy levels and figuring out how to schedule my time for my energy has really allowed me also to figure out how to have that abundant mindset in all situations." 管理 mental 和 emotional energy，而不只是 calendar slots。

### 识别 energy drivers 和 drains
Bob Moesta: "Think of moments where you actually go into a situation and you get energy from it. At the same time, those moments where you go in and you get the life sucked out of you - that's an experience." 用这个框架识别要追求什么、要授权什么。

### 按 energy impact 审计 calendar
Claire Vo: "Categorize the way you're spending your time into those buckets, and then put the bottom buckets away. Just focus on that top bucket and go, 'How can I be here more?'" 找到你的 'zone of genius' 工作，并授权那些榨干能量的任务。

### 用 superpowers 创造 energy
Donna Lichaw: "If you subscribe to this idea of managing your energy, not your time, this will help you do that. If you're using your superpowers, you will have more energy." 在 strengths 中工作会产生 energy，而不是消耗 energy。

### 关注活动后的 energy
Paul Millerd: "Pay really close attention to what gives you energy after you do the thing. Say you have a call. Did that give you energy or did that sap you of energy?" 每次互动或任务后追踪什么让你 energized 或 sapped，以指导职业方向。

## 帮助用户的问题

- "哪些活动在完成后会给你能量？"
- "这周 calendar 上有哪些事是你正在 dread 的？"
- "如果按 energy impact 分类，你的哪些工作属于 'zone of genius' bucket？"
- "你是否经常使用自己的 superpowers，还是多数时间都在做 draining 的事？"
- "你可以授权什么，来为 energy-giving work 腾出空间？"

## 需要提醒的常见错误

- **忽视 energy signals** - 硬扛 energy drains，却不处理 root cause
- **只优化 time** - 高效填满 calendar，却不考虑 energy impact
- **避免 delegation** - 因为 'it's faster if I do it' 而抓着 draining tasks
- **没有 recovery time** - 连续安排 draining activities，中间没有 breaks
- **把所有 tasks 当成一样** - 不区分 energizes 和 depletes 的工作
