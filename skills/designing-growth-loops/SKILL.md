# Designing Growth Loops

使用来自 54 位产品领导者的框架，帮助用户设计有效的 growth loops；这些领导者曾在 Dropbox、LinkedIn、Calendly 等公司构建 viral 和 product-led growth engines。

## 如何提供帮助

当用户请求 growth loops 相关帮助时：

1. **识别 loop 类型** - 判断他们需要 viral、paid、content，还是 product-led acquisition loops
2. **评估 prerequisites** - 检查他们是否具备支撑 loop 的 LTV、network effects 或 product stickiness
3. **找到自然 sharing moment** - 帮助识别用户自然想把别人带进来的时刻
4. **为 compounding 设计** - 确保 loop 能反馈到自身，形成可持续增长

## 核心原则

### 一个 dominant loop 最重要
Luc Levesque: "It's usually just one loop that you need to get right. Most successful companies scale primarily through one dominant, well-executed growth loop." 在多元化前，先识别并主导一个 primary channel。

### Viral growth 是可学习的科学
Nikita Bier: "With certainty, if you're good at your job, you can make an app grow and go viral. Over the years of building all these apps, I've accrued all these growth hacks that still nobody knows about." 基于平台特定机制，建立自己的 growth tactics library。

### LTV 解锁 paid loops
Yuriy Timen: "If you have really healthy LTVs, then there is a big opportunity to play paid and lean into paid growth loops." 计算 single-player LTV 是否足够高，能否支撑可持续 paid acquisition。

### Product-led acquisition 拥有零 marginal cost
Julian Shapiro: "By me trying to use PayPal in its everyday intention, I'm automatically enticing someone else to become a PayPal customer." PLA 是最可扩展的 channel，因为它完全在公司控制范围内。

### 快速市场中，innovation 胜过 optimization
Elena Verna: "I feel like only 30 to 40% of what I've learned transfers here because we need to invest in such bigger bets and innovate and create new growth loops." 在快速变化的 AI categories 中，要从 95% optimization 转向 95% innovation。

### Word-of-mouth 需要 frequency
Uri Levine: "Word-of-mouth you can only have if you have high frequency of use. If you're using Waze every day, then every day you have an opportunity to tell someone else." 可持续 word-of-mouth 与用户使用频率紧密相关。

### 用定性和定量方式 map loops
Ben Williams: "Being able to identify the various micro and macro loops, how they're all connected, being able to document them in a qualitative model provides a shared understanding and guides intentional investment." 记录 micro 和 macro loops 及其连接方式，形成共享理解。

### 40-50% organic 是健康比例
Gokul Rajaram: "A good metric is that 40 to 50% of your new customers should ideally come from organic channels. If 90% come from paid, at some point the music is going to stop."

## 帮助用户的问题

- "用户自然想把别人带进来的时刻是什么？"
- "当更多人使用时，你的产品会变得更有价值吗？"
- "你的增长中 organic vs paid 各占多少？"
- "你的 customer LTV 和 payback period 是多少？"
- "有没有某个用户动作会自动把产品暴露给其他人？"
- "你能识别用户已经自然分享产品的位置吗？"

## 需要提醒的常见错误

- **Freemium 上做 paid acquisition** - 依赖 paid acquisition 的 consumer subscriptions 通常会可预测地失败
- **强行制造 network effects** - Network effects 通常是内生的，很难事后补上
- **过早多元化** - 早期公司应聚焦一个有效 engine
- **Single-channel dependency** - 后期公司如果 90%+ 依赖单一 channel，风险极高
- **没有 organic WOM 的 referral programs** - Referrals 会放大已有 word-of-mouth，但不能凭空创造它
