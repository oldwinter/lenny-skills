# Engineering Culture

使用来自 19 位产品领导者的框架，帮助用户建设强 engineering culture；这些领导者曾构建高绩效 engineering organizations。

## 如何提供帮助

当用户请求 engineering culture 相关帮助时：

1. **诊断当前状态** - 理解 team size、practices 和 pain points
2. **识别 bottleneck** - 判断问题出在 DevEx、org structure、talent，还是 process
3. **围绕正确 outcomes 设计** - 聚焦 speed、stability 和 developer satisfaction
4. **平衡 structure 和 autonomy** - 帮助校准需要多少 process

## 核心原则

### DevOps 是 capabilities，不是 tools
Nicole Forsgren: "DevOps is not a toolchain you buy. Marketing teams labeled toolchains DevOps because they wanted your money. DevOps is a set of capabilities - technical capabilities, architectural capabilities, cultural capabilities, and lean management practices." 聚焦 integrated capabilities，而不只是实施特定 tools。

### Conway's Law 很有力量
Dhanji R. Prasanna: "Conway's Law can be really, really powerful. You ship your org structure. What you're organized as in terms of teams and collaborating groups matters a lot to what you build." 组织结构会直接塑造 architecture 和 product quality。

### Engineers 正在成为 architects
Scott Wu: "We've thought about Devin as really allowing engineers to go from bricklayer to architect. A lot of it is just getting to the point where you can do the high-level directing and specify things exactly how you want." 工程未来会让人类从手工 coding 转向 architectural oversight。

### DevEx 是基础
Nicole Forsgren: "Developer Experience is what it's like to build software, day to day. The friction they face, the workflows they have to go through. It's important because when DevEx is poor, everything else just isn't going to help." Flow state、cognitive load 和 feedback loops 是三大支柱。

### 优化 clock speed
Albert Cheng: "The product experience of Duolingo actually changes multiple times per day for each user. They care a lot about the clock speed of the company." 高绩效 engineering culture 围绕极高 experimentation frequency 建立。

### 要求所有 functions 接触 code
Brandon Chu: "All project management was just in GitHub, just commenting on PRs, and even marketers in order to upload a blog post, you'd have to commit and deploy it." 通过要求所有 functions 直接接触 engineering tools 来建立技术文化。

### 高 talent density 减少 process 需求
Michael Truell: "We've been able to get away with not a ton of process yet on the engineering front by hiring people who are really excellent, level-headed, people who are less focused on external validation, more focused on building something really great." 通过招聘 disposition 合适的人，降低组织 overhead。

### Pair programming 被低估
Farhan Thawar: "Pair programming is the most underutilized management tool in engineering, bar none. The throughput limiter is not hands-on keyboard. It's where is the good elegant solution?" 用 pairing 做知识传递，并找到 elegant solutions。

## 帮助用户的问题

- "developers 日常 workflow 里最大的 friction point 是什么？"
- "你的 org structure 如何映射到 product architecture？"
- "Engineers 有多少时间处在 flow state，而不是 meetings？"
- "你们团队的 'clock speed' 是什么：多久能 ship 并测试变化？"
- "Non-engineering roles 理解 deployment workflow 吗？"

## 需要提醒的常见错误

- **购买 tools，而不是建设 capabilities** - 期待 DevOps toolchain 修复文化问题
- **忽视 Conway's Law** - Org structure 与期望 product architecture 不匹配
- **忽略 DevEx** - 糟糕 developer experience 会削弱其他所有投入
- **给高 talent teams 过度加 process** - 给优秀团队增加不必要 bureaucracy
- **Code quality over customer value** - 优先追求完美 architecture，而不是解决用户问题
