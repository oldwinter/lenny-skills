# Partnership & BD

使用来自 9 位产品领导者的洞察，帮助用户建立 strategic partnerships 和 business development deals。

## 如何提供帮助

当用户请求 partnerships 或 BD 相关帮助时：

1. **理解 opportunity** - 询问潜在 partner、双方各自想要什么，以及理想 outcome 是什么
2. **评估 network vs. merit dynamics** - 判断 success 取决于 relationships 还是 product quality，并据此规划
3. **设计 approach** - 帮助他们打磨 pitch，展示 mutual benefit，而不只是自己的 needs
4. **推进 negotiation** - 指导 timing、leverage，以及何时 say no

## 核心原则

### 某些行业的 partnerships 依赖 network
Ayo Omojola: "Very often the way to make things happen is network-dependent, not necessarily about the merit of the thing itself. There are companies that exist because the founders know the CEO of every major payer." 在 healthcare、finance 或 enterprise 等行业，relationships 往往比 product 更重要。尽早判断你的 business 是否 network-dependent。

### 把自己定位成 platform 的 case study
Adam Grenier: "Let me into your alpha, because I have five years of experience buying mobile ads. If you get us to work, we're a killer case study for a category you want to grow." 与 major platforms 合作时，展示你的 success 如何帮助它们证明 business model。主动成为新 features 的 alpha tester。

### 构建 scalable platforms，而不是 bespoke deals
Keith Yandell: "We'll build you a product so you won't need to ask us every time. Then you'll know the parameters in which you can negotiate." 有效 BD teams 会与 Product 合作，为 partners 创建 self-serve platforms，而不是谈判一次性 integrations。这会提高 velocity 并减少 engineering load。

### Strategic patience 带来更好 deals
Jason Droege: "McDonald's approached us and I said 'No.' I pushed them off for four or five months. It ended up being a net benefit because we got a great deal." 不要因为 partner 体量大就急着进入 partnership。初始 resistance 可以带来更好 terms 或 exclusivity。

### Channel partners 不会像 founders 一样销售
Jen Abel: "The problem with channel partnerships is there are a hundred of you on the list and you're expecting them to sell on your behalf. They're not visionaries, they're consultants." 不要期待 Accenture 或 Deloitte 为你的 product 做 evangelize。他们没有 founder 的 vision-casting ability。

### 用 corp dev 找 internal sponsors
Julia Schottenstein: "Use that corp dev team to your advantage. Take that meeting, say you're not interested in acquisition yet, but push them for an introduction to someone who could sponsor the deal - usually someone in Product or a GM." 即使你不想出售公司，corp dev teams 也是通往 internal sponsors 的入口。

### 在 need moment 找 partners
Meltem Kuran: "VCs ask portfolio companies 'What platform do you trust for hiring?' So people go to their VCs for that kind of question." 最好的 partners 是目标 audience 在问题出现的准确时刻已经会咨询的人。

### 把 partnerships 当作 iterated prisoner's dilemma
Tobi Lutke: "Being a good partner is like a corporate marshmallow test companies tend to fail. The correct way to play iterated prisoner's dilemma is coordinate for both sides - it's way more valuable over long periods." 抵制以牺牲 partner 为代价提前拿走 future profits。长期 coordination 永远胜过短期 defection。

### 构建前先用 manual operations 测试
Keith Yandell: "We should have tested this at one hotel with hacky operations before asking for product resources." 在投入 engineering time 前，用 manual operations 验证 partnership hypotheses。Dream big, start small。

## 帮助用户的问题

- "partner 想从这笔 deal 中得到什么？对他们有什么好处？"
- "这个 industry 是 network-dependent，还是 product merit 会胜出？"
- "你能把自己定位成他们想证明的某件事的 case study 吗？"
- "你的 leverage 是什么？你是否能承受 say no？"
- "你是在走向 scalable platform，还是在谈 one-off deal？"
- "partner organization 内部谁可以 sponsor 这件事？"

## 需要提醒的常见错误

- **期待 partners 替你销售** - Channel partners 和 consultants 不会像 founders 一样 evangelize。不要外包 vision-casting
- **急着进入 big-name partnerships** - Strategic patience 往往带来更好 deal terms。不要让 FOMO 驱动糟糕 deals
- **验证前就构建** - 先用 manual operations 测试 partnership ideas，再投入 engineering resources
- **忽视 network dynamics** - 某些行业 relationships 比 product 更重要。知道自己在玩哪种 game
- **短期 defection** - 牺牲 partner 提前拿走 profits 会摧毁长期价值。Play the long game
