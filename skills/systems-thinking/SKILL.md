# Systems Thinking

使用来自 6 位产品领导者的框架和洞察，帮助用户把 systems thinking 应用于 complex problems。

## 如何提供帮助

当用户请求 systems thinking 相关帮助时：

1. **Map the system** - 帮助他们识别所有 players、各自 incentives，以及他们如何相互作用
2. **识别 stocks and flows** - 理解什么会累积（stocks），什么会在 states 之间移动（flows）
3. **追踪 second-order effects** - 推演任何 change 的 first-order impact 之后会发生什么
4. **寻找 leverage points** - 识别 small interventions 能创造 large systemic changes 的位置

## 核心原则

### See the system
Seth Godin: "What does it mean to be a strategic thinker? It means to see the system." 理解那些支配 products 和 organizations 成败的 invisible rules、culture 和 interoperability，是 strategic thinking 的基础。

### 思考所有 players 和 incentives
Sriram: "Systems thinking. Think of all the players in the system, think of all of their incentives and how they interact with each other." 这种 approach 在处理 complex product trade-offs 和 multi-agent incentives 时，比 Jobs-to-be-Done 更适合。

### 使用 stocks and flows
Will Larson: "Systems thinking is basically you try to think about stocks and flows. Stocks are things that accumulate and flows are the movement from a stock to another thing." 用这个 framework 建模 hiring pipelines 或 user funnels 等 business processes。

### 考虑 second、third 和 fourth-order effects
Hari Srinivasan: "The skillsets that you think through and manage in a complicated ecosystem are quite different." 管理 complex ecosystems 要理解那些超越 immediate impact、层层 cascade 的 effects。

### 思考今天 decisions 之外的影响
Nickey Skarstad: "Second order thinking is you being able to think beyond the decisions that you're making today." 考虑 current decisions 如何影响 future constraints 和 ecosystem dynamics。

### Automate recurring pains
Melissa Perri + Denise Tilles: "Tell me about some process you really hated and ended up trying to automate or build a system around to make it better." 识别 recurring manual pains，并 build automated systems 或 frameworks 来解决它们。

## 帮助用户的问题

- "这个 system 中所有 players 是谁？每个人想要什么？"
- "如果你做这个 change，接下来会发生什么？再之后又会发生什么？"
- "这个 system 中什么会随时间累积（stocks），什么会在 states 之间 flow？"
- "feedback loops 在哪里？包括 reinforcing 和 balancing loops。"
- "如果移除哪个 constraint，会 unlock 这个 system 中最多 value？"
- "哪个 recurring manual pain 可以被 systematized？"

## 需要提醒的常见错误

- **Only seeing first-order effects** - Changes 会以并不立刻明显的方式在 systems 中 ripple
- **Ignoring incentives** - System 中每个 player 都会响应自己的 incentives，而不是你的
- **Optimizing locally** - 改善 system 的一个部分，可能让整体更糟
- **Missing feedback loops** - 很多 systems 有 self-reinforcing 或 self-balancing dynamics，会 amplify 或 dampen changes
- **Treating symptoms instead of causes** - Systems problems 往往需要处理 root causes，而不是 visible symptoms
