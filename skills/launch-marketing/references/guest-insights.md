## Anton Osika

## Arielle Jackson
*Arielle Jackson*

> "For early stage startups, we're almost always running the launch announcement as an exclusive, which means you give the news to a single outlet. And they're the only ones who get to write about it."

**洞察：** 面向 startups 的 modern PR 往往需要向 single outlet 提供 exclusive，而不是 broad embargoed release。

**战术建议：**
- 识别一个报道你 specific stage companies 的 single target outlet
- 在 launch announcement 前准备好 website 承接 traffic

*时间戳：01:11:19*

---

> "Don't do a straight funding announcement. A lot of founders raise money and they're like, cool, let's announce. It's like, no, we'll use that funding announcement as a news hook to tell a larger story and a larger story might be your products available. You have reference customers, you have momentum."

**洞察：** Funding 是 news hook，不是 story 本身；应利用它突出 product availability 或 customer momentum。

**战术建议：**
- 将 funding news 与 product launch 或 partnership announcement 组合
- 聚焦让 story 对 outlet readers 有趣，而不只是对 company 有意义

*时间戳：01:12:08*

---

> "Don't sleep on local press. If you have a local business or a local story or local customers... We turned all of our customers into heroes and went after local press."

**洞察：** 对有 regional impact 或 physical customers 的 startups，local press 可能更 accessible 且 effective。

**战术建议：**
- Pitch local outlets 时，突出 business 如何帮助 local heroes 或 customers
- 将 company story 与 hiring 或 economic shifts 等 local trends 连接

*时间戳：01:14:23*

- 对 engineers 有 purchasing latitude 的 platform products，使用 developer-led GTM

## Christine Itwaru
*Christine Itwaru*

**洞察：** In-product education 和 content 应被视为任何 feature release 的 'definition of done' core part。

**战术建议：**
- 将 in-app guides 或 educational content 的创建纳入 feature development checklist
- 与 product marketing 合作，develop 通过 in-app content driving engagement 的 playbooks

## Emily Kramer

## Geoffrey Moore
*Geoffrey Moore*

> "The Crossing the Chasm model is, 'Can you create a viable repeatable business?' And in order to do that, you need to have an ecosystem of partners work with you in order to consolidate your position. Well, why would an ecosystem work with some startup that nobody's ever heard of? The answer would be if you had consolidated a market segment where you were number one."

**洞察：** GTM success 依赖在 small segment 成为 market leader，从而吸引 scaling 所需的 ecosystem partners。

**战术建议：**
- 聚焦 single beachhead segment，成为 partners 想支持的 'big fish'。
- 相比 broad、shallow adoption，优先 niche 中的 market share。

*时间戳：00:07:23*

---

> "In the early market, you know you're there because first of all, the story is the technology... In the bowling alley one then the playbook is, 'No, where's the problem?'... In the tornado... this is when you do want sales coverage, and you do want to go broad."

**洞察：** 随着 category mature，Go-to-market playbooks 必须从 technology-centric 转向 problem-centric，再转向 market-share-centric。

**战术建议：**
- 对 visionaries 使用 'project model'，对 pragmatists 使用 'solution model'。
- 只有当 market 进入 'tornado' phase 后，才转向 horizontal sales coverage。

*时间戳：00:44:08*
