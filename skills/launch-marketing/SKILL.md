# Launch Marketing

使用来自 26 位产品领导者的策略，帮助用户规划并执行有效 product launches。

## 如何提供帮助

当用户请求 product launch 相关帮助时：

1. **理解 launch 类型** - 询问这是 company launch、funding announcement、major feature，还是 incremental release。不同类型需要不同方法
2. **澄清 goals** - 判断 primary objective：customer acquisition、recruiting、fundraising、feedback，还是 partnership opportunities
3. **评估 readiness** - 检查基础是否就绪：positioning、website ready for traffic、internal teams 已对 messaging briefed
4. **设计 approach** - 帮助他们在 exclusive press、social media campaigns、Product Hunt、community seeding 或组合方案中选择

## 核心原则

### 把 press pitch 成 exclusive，而不是 broad embargo
Arielle Jackson: "For early stage startups, we're almost always running the launch announcement as an exclusive, which means you give the news to a single outlet." 识别一个覆盖你所处阶段公司的 target outlet。一次只向一个 reporter 提供 exclusive story。

### Funding 是 news hook，不是 story 本身
Arielle Jackson: "Don't do a straight funding announcement. Use that funding as a news hook to tell a larger story - your product's available, you have reference customers, you have momentum." 把 funding news 与 product launch 或 partnership 结合，才会对 readers 有趣。

### 分离 soft launch 和 hard launch
Janna Bastow: "Decoupling the technical release (soft launch) from the marketing event (hard launch) reduces stress and improves marketing quality." 先 soft launch，收集 functional videos 和 testimonials。利用两次 launch 之间的时间，基于最终产品规划高影响 campaign。

### 找到 "sizzle" feature
Bret Taylor: "Satellite imagery wasn't the most important part of Google Maps, but it was the sizzle to the steak and it created a viral moment." 识别一个视觉上令人印象深刻或适合分享的 feature 来制造 buzz，即使它不是 primary use case。

### 使用 lightning strikes，而不是 peanut butter
Christopher Lochhead: "What if you launched like Hollywood launches a movie? I'd rather matter for one week a year than be irrelevant for the rest of the year." 把 marketing efforts 集中到每年 1-2 个大型事件，而不是把 budget 薄薄摊开。让目标 audience 在短窗口内无法忽视你。

### 以 concentric circles 分发
Lulu Cheng Meservey: "You go out in concentric circles starting from your own desk. First get clear on your message, then co-founders, executives, employees, investors, power users, and out from there." 先确保内圈 fully aligned，再进入下一圈。Internal alignment 会保护 external credibility。

### 把 product seed 给正确 influencers
Lulu Cheng Meservey: "Find the Venn diagram of people who will be obsessed with this product AND have a large following among your target audience. Shower them with free product." 面向高 product affinity 和高 audience influence 的交集做 organic evangelism。

### PR 是为了 credibility，不是 leads
Zoelle Egner: "PR is not going to get you leads or users. What it is good for is credibility - hiring or improving the response rate for your cold outbound." 把 press coverage 用作 recruiting emails 和 sales outreach 里的 social proof。不要依赖它直接获客。

### 把 press coverage 当作 marketing fuel
Jason Feifer: "Sometimes the point isn't to reach the readers at all. Put money behind promoting the tweet about your coverage to the people you want to notice you got coverage." 网站上的 "As seen in" logo 可能比文章 direct traffic 更有价值。

### 建立 launch cadence，而不是一次 big bang
Zoelle Egner: "Instead of one big launch, have a series of launches that allow you to stay top of mind and create momentum. Audiences respond to novelty." 每隔几个月规划 launches，重新激活 communities 并保持 presence。

### PR prep 提前六周开始
Emilie Gerber: "Before an announcement, you want to kick off six weeks in advance - working on the blog, media training, outreach, getting investor quotes." 优先 reporter availability，而不是内部 deadline。为了获得高质量 coverage，日期要灵活。

### 为 algorithm momentum 优化
Chris Hutchins: "Ranking charts are driven by momentum of new subscribers, not total volume. Launch with multiple pieces of content to build early momentum." 对 podcast、Product Hunt 等 platform-based launches，在短窗口内集中活动，触发 algorithmic boost。

## 帮助用户的问题

- "这次 launch 的 primary goal 是 customers、recruiting、fundraising，还是 feedback？"
- "你是否已经有一个 exclusive 目标 outlet，还是准备 broad outreach？"
- "你的 'sizzle' feature 是什么，什么东西别人会分享，即使它不是 core value？"
- "Website 准备好承接 traffic 了吗？Internal teams 是否已 briefed on messaging？"
- "哪些 influencers 同时满足 '会爱这个产品' 和 '拥有你的 target audience'？"
- "你有多少 lead time？Press 理想情况是 6 周。"

## 需要提醒的常见错误

- **一次 launch 太多东西** - Kevin Weil on Libra: "The whole world went 'Oh my God, that's a lot of change at once.'" Radical innovations 要一次介绍一个
- **Pitch press 时没有 exclusive offer** - 给很多 outlets 的 generic pitches 会被忽略。一次向一个 reporter 提供 exclusive
- **把 funding 当成 story** - Funding 是 hook，不是 news。把它与 product 或 customer momentum 结合
- **依赖 PR 做 user acquisition** - Press 建立 credibility，但很少直接带来 signups。用它支持 recruiting 和 sales enablement
- **Rigid launch dates** - 优先 reporter availability，而不是内部 timelines。有灵活日期比没有 coverage 更好
