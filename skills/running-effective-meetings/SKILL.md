# Running Effective Meetings

使用来自 40 位产品领导者的框架，帮助用户开更有效的 meetings。

## 如何提供帮助

当用户请求 meetings 相关帮助时：

1. **质疑 meeting 必要性** - 询问这是否可以 async，或者 meeting 是否真的需要
2. **澄清 purpose** - 判断它是为了 discovery、discussion，还是 decision
3. **设计 structure** - 帮助创建合适 agenda 和 process
4. **确保 follow-through** - 指导如何记录 decisions 和 next steps

## 核心原则

### 分离 strategic 和 operational meetings
Naomi Gleit: "We have one weekly sort of strategic meeting. It's more open-ended, there is time for discussion... We also have one weekly operational meeting, which is highly structured where we go through all of the priority projects." 不要把 unstructured strategic discussion 和 structured operational updates 混在一起。

### Meetings 只用于 discussion
Annie Duke: "People generally think the purpose of a meeting is for three things, discover, discuss, decide. The only thing that's ever supposed to happen in a meeting is the discussion part." Discovery 和 decision-making 应该 async 完成。同步时间只留给真正 dialogue。

### 用三个问题结束
Alisa Cohn: "My three questions to end the meeting are, what did we decide here? Who needs to do what by when? And who else needs to know?" 用标准化问题结束 meetings，确保 alignment 并避免 re-meetings。

### 不要跳过 priming phase
Evan LaPointe: "Meetings, generally speaking, are a combination of priming and decision making... A lot of meetings skip the priming step altogether." 大多数 meeting dysfunction 来自跳过 context-setting。在进入 decisions 前，显式包含 priming phase。

### 为重要 decisions 清空 calendar
Jake Knapp + John Zeratsky: "The big idea with a design Sprint is to go from a zero to a prototype and a test of that prototype in just five days. And it's a recipe, it's a scripted set of activities." High-stakes strategic decisions 适合 time-boxed、calendar-cleared workshops。

### Ruthlessly minimize meetings
Gibson Biddle: "Minimize meetings, okay? Minimize meetings. That sucks the life out of everybody, including you." 主动移除 non-essential meetings 来保护 productivity。满 calendar 是 shame badge，不是 honor。

### State changes 维持 engagement
Wes Kao: "The state change method is that you should punctuate your monologues with state changes. So state changes are anything that shakes your audience awake." 在 virtual meetings 中，每 3-5 分钟用 polls、chat prompts 或 speaker changes 打断 monologues。

### Pre-reads 把 status updates 转为 async
Matt Mochary: "If everyone pre-prepared their update... we could take a three hour meeting down to a 45 minute meeting." 要求 attendees 会前提交 written updates 和 proposed solutions。会议开头先 silent reading。

## 帮助用户的问题

- "这个 meeting 必须发生吗，还是可以 async 解决？"
- "这个 meeting 的 purpose 是 discovery、discussion，还是 decision？"
- "Attendees 会前应该完成什么 pre-work？"
- "Decision-maker 是谁？所有人都知道吗？"
- "Meeting 后你如何 capture 和 communicate decisions？"
- "什么条件成立时，你可以 cancel 这个 recurring meeting？"

## 需要提醒的常见错误

- **Status update meetings** - 用同步时间传递本可 async 共享的信息
- **No clear purpose** - Meetings 没有 explicit objectives
- **Missing priming** - 没建立 shared context 就直接进入 decisions
- **No documented outcomes** - 结束时没有记录 decided what 和 who's doing what
- **Too many attendees** - 每多一个人都会降低 candor 并增加 cost
