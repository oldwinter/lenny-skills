# Having Difficult Conversations

使用来自 43 位产品领导者的框架，帮助用户以勇气和技巧处理 tough conversations。

## 如何提供帮助

当用户请求 difficult conversation 相关帮助时：

1. **理解情境** - 询问是哪类 conversation（feedback、performance、firing、conflict resolution、delivering bad news），以及对他们来说困难在哪里
2. **检查准备程度** - 确保他们有具体 examples、清晰 desired outcome，并思考过对方视角
3. **选择合适 framework** - 将情境匹配到合适结构，例如 SBI、CORE、NVC 或 Radical Candor
4. **必要时 role-play** - 主动练习对话，让他们听到表达效果并预判 reactions

## 核心原则

### Radical Candor：Care personally AND challenge directly
Kim Scott: "Radical Candor is what happens when you care personally and challenge directly at the same time." 避免 obnoxious aggression（challenge without care）、ruinous empathy（care without challenge）和 manipulative insincerity（neither）。多数人会倾向 ruinous empathy：为了照顾感受而 withholding feedback，实际上会伤害对方。

### 使用 Situation-Behavior-Impact framework
Paige Costello: "Situation, behavior, impact. The situation is on Tuesday in that meeting at 3:00. Behavior, you interrupted me while I was saying this thing. Impact, made me feel like you weren't listening." 保持客观具体。描述 camera 会记录到的行为，而不是解释对方动机。

### 区分 feelings 和 attributions
Carole Robin: "'I feel that you don't care' and 'I feel you're being insensitive' are not feelings. That's where we make our biggest mistakes." 使用真实 emotion words（hurt、disappointed、anxious），不要用 "I feel that" 或 "I feel like" 引入故事和指控。

### Stay on your side of the net
Carole Robin: "Stick with the two realities you know - your intent and their behavior. We get in trouble the minute we think we know the other person's reality." 只谈你观察到的事和它对你的影响。不要假设对方 motives。

### 在 pinches 变成 crunches 前处理
Carole Robin: "Address it while it's still small and then it won't get big." 小的人际 irritations 被忽视后会升级成重大冲突。注意自己何时被 activated，并尽早开口。

### Termination news 要亲自传达，绝不要用 email
Matt Mochary: "The biggest marker between a botched layoff and a successful layoff is whether they heard it from their manager in a one-on-one." 始终在 private 1:1 meeting 中传达 termination news。确保场景允许对方私下表达 emotions。

### 在 firing conversation 前给出 warning
Alisa Cohn: "I need you to fix this within the next 30 days. Otherwise, we're going to have to part ways." 解雇前必须有一次 "crystal clear" conversation，明确说明 consequences。使用 "part ways" 或 "not have a future together" 这样的表达，避免 ambiguity。

### 分离 decision 和 implementation
Matt Mochary: "Decision is one thing, implementation is completely separate. If you let them go kindly, become their agent - help them find their next job actively." Business decision 也许必要，但 implementation 应该 compassionate。主动联系你的 network，帮对方找到适合的位置。

### 传达坏消息时提供 hope
Alisa Cohn: "Hope for the future is so important. 'I know this is challenging to hear, not going to promote you, but I want you to know I'm committed to your success here.'" 拒绝晋升或传达失望消息时，承认困难，表达对其职业发展的 commitment，并提供具体支持。

### 问 "What did you hear me say?"
Carole Robin: "One of the most powerful things you can do when somebody responds unexpectedly is ask 'What did you hear me say?'" 通过检查 perception mismatch 来修复对话。即使那不是你的 intent，也要基于对方听到的内容来 validate 其反应。

### Withholding feedback 是自私
Matt MacInnis: "The most selfish thing you can do is withhold feedback from someone. You're optimizing for your own comfort." 把 difficult feedback 重构为服务对方的无私行为。观察到能帮助对方改进的事，就及时给出。

### 用 "nevertheless" 结束争论
Annie Duke: "'I hear you and understand. Nevertheless, this is what's going to happen.' Validate the perspective, then use 'nevertheless' to transition to the final decision without inviting further debate."

## 帮助用户的问题

- "你需要处理的具体 behavior 是什么？camera 会记录到什么？"
- "你希望这次 conversation 达成什么 outcome？"
- "你以前给过这个 feedback 吗？对方是否 crystal clear 地知道不改变会发生什么？"
- "这次 conversation 对你来说困难在哪里？你害怕什么？"
- "对方可能如何以不同方式理解这个 situation？"
- "你和这个人的 relationship foundation 是什么，他们知道你 care about them 吗？"

## 需要提醒的常见错误

- **Ruinous empathy** - 太 "nice" 以至于不说 hard truth。从长期看，这会更伤害对方
- **"I feel that..." phrasing** - 这是把 accusations 伪装成 feelings。使用真实 emotions：hurt、anxious、frustrated
- **Assuming intent** - 你不知道对方为什么这么做。只谈 behavior 和 impact
- **把 feedback 留到 reviews** - Feedback 应该 immediate and ongoing，而不是攒到季度对话
- **没有提前 warning 就 firing** - 真正 firing 应该是简短确认，而不是惊喜。对方应先听过 "crystal clear" warning
- **用 email 或 group announcements 传达 terminations** - 始终在 private 1:1 中亲自传达
